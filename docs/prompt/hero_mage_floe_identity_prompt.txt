ART STYLE (project style bible — do not deviate):
hand-drawn cartoon game sprite, thick near-black outlines (pure black or very dark warm
brown — never a mid-tone or colorful outline), flat saturated fills with light cel shading
(one highlight tone, one shadow tone), no gradients, no texture noise, no painterly
rendering, no photographic detail, 3/4 top-down camera matching a tactics grid.

PROPORTIONS — check against this, drawing them too tall is the most common failure:
- Extreme super-deformed mascot. The HEAD INCLUDING HAIR OR HELMET is about HALF the
  character's total height. Body, arms and legs together are the other half: short,
  stubby, chunky.
- The whole character fits in a roughly SQUARE box — total width is about 0.9 to 1.0
  times total height. If it looks like a slim standing person, it is wrong.
- This is NOT a 2-head or 2.5-head chibi. Think 1.5-head mascot: giant head, tiny body.

PROPS may extend beyond the body — an upright spear, a tall staff, horns or a plume are all
fine and will not be cropped in game. The square-box rule above is measured on the BODY
(top of the head down to the feet), not on the props. Props should still be big in MASS,
not just long and thin, so they survive at small size.

SIMPLIFY — characterise with a few bold strokes and colours, never by piling on detail.
At the size this is displayed, detail becomes noise and makes the character read smaller:
- Only 4 to 6 flat colours on the whole character. ONE of them dominates the body and is
  that character's identity colour.
- NO belts, straps, buckles, pouches, rivets, armour seams, laces, stitching, patterns,
  engraving or trim. Delete every small accessory.
- Face is minimal: two simple dark eyes, two eyebrows, at most a tiny mouth line. No nose,
  no ear detail, no blush.
- Arms and legs are simple rounded stubs; hands are mitten shapes, no fingers.
- The signature prop is ONE big clean simple silhouette with no ornament on it.
- The character must read as a handful of bold flat shapes, not as an illustration.

BRIGHTNESS AND VALUE RANGE — this is what separates a cheerful palette from a muddy
one, and it is the most common failure:
- The palette must be BRIGHT and span a wide value range. Saturated mid-tones plus a
  genuine near-white highlight.
- Every character carries ONE clearly light area — cream, off-white or white (cloak,
  collar, sash, plume, mount) — covering roughly 10-15% of the body. This is what the
  eye locks onto at small size.
- Never render a character in uniformly dark or muted tones.
- Avoid grey, avoid dusty, chalky or desaturated colors. Avoid olive-drab and khaki.
- Cream highlight anchors: #F0F0E0, #E0E0D0, #F0E0C0. Warm gold accent: #F0C060.

The sprite must stay readable at 40 pixels tall: silhouette first, exaggerate the
weapon and the one signature feature, drop everything that needs zoom to appreciate.
NO TEXT, no labels, no captions, no watermark anywhere in the image.

MATCH THE ATTACHED EXISTING UNIT SPRITES EXACTLY in line weight, head-to-body ratio,
stubby limbs, face language, and simplification. This must look like she belongs on
the same tactics board as the attached mage / priest / swordsman. Do not make a
pretty illustration, anime girl, or fashion design sheet.

CRITICAL BACKGROUND: 100% solid flat MAGENTA hex #FF00FF filling the entire image.
No white, no grey, no ground shadow, no drop shadow, no gradient.

CAMERA: 3/4 view from slightly above, full body, idle facing slightly toward the camera.
Same camera as the attached mage token.

COLORS AND POSE ONLY (do not change the art system):
- Identity color: ice-blue robe #3EC8E0 covering most of the tiny body.
- Cream collar #F0F0E0 (the one light anchor).
- Hair: chunky honey-gold #F0C84C, long enough to read as long hair but drawn as 3–4
  big rounded masses, not strands. Same hair language as the swordsman's blocky hair.
- Skin #EEC49A. Simple round brown shoes.
- Head decoration: THREE chunky ice-blue triangles on the forehead hairline, one shape.
  Not a tall wizard hat (that is Aoli). Not a detailed crown.
- Ice hem: the robe bottom is 4 big downward triangles (icicles), same bold geometry

6 colors total: ice blue, cream, gold hair, skin, book brown, outline.
