# 设计依据：docs/敌人图鉴.md §2 第 4 章。剪影语法沿用第一章（圆滚/宽伞/横长/穹顶
# 对应 sword/bow/cavalry/shield），换题材与配色。
#
# 这一章的配色是全五章最难排的，推演记在这里：
#
#   「毒」的自然联想是病态紫，但**紫做不出来**：抠色键是品红 #FF00FF，H270–330 的主色
#   到键色的色距 < 150，会被连着素材一起吃掉（docs/美术管线-AI-sprite.md §2，魂晶踩过）。
#   所以这一章的毒感只能靠**酸黄绿 + 近黑**的明度两极来表达。
#
#   而黄绿这一带正好被我方两个职业夹住：弓手 H90 L33（叶绿）、骑兵 H42 L52（金黄）。
#   H42–H90 之间任何中等明度的黄绿都会同时贴着两个，色相差永远不够 25°。
#   突破口是**明度**（判据是「色相差 ≥25° 或明度差 ≥20」，满足一条即可）：
#
#   1 泥沼手  #24211C L12 近黑      vs 全部职业明度差 ≥ 15，且是全场最暗的一块
#   2 吹箭虫  #D2DC7A H68 S55 L67  vs 弓手 H90 L33 明度差 34✓ / 骑兵 H42 L52 色相差 26✓
#                                   刻意取**亮**酸黄绿而不是中等脓黄——中等值两条判据都不过
#   3 沼行鳄  #3D6B3D H120 S27 L33 vs 弓手 30°✓ / 法师 H180 60°✓ / 沼泽 H60 60°✓
#   4 泥壳蟹  #A8B0A0 H100 S9 L66  vs 骑兵 58°✓ / 弓手 明度差 33✓；低饱和，靠饱和度轴和吹箭虫分开
#
#   明度铺成 12 / 67 / 33 / 66。本章地形沼泽 H60 L24、河流 H207 L54 都偏暗偏中，
#   两只亮的（吹箭虫、泥壳蟹）浮得出来，两只暗的靠色相和地形错开。
#
# 禁色：粉、品红、紫罗兰——这一章尤其要盯，因为「毒」很容易被模型画成紫。
#

# mob-1 泥沼手(sword) / mob-2 吹箭虫(bow) / mob-3 沼行鳄(cavalry) / mob-4 泥壳蟹(shield)

A 2-column x 2-row grid of 4 CARTOON SWAMP CREATURE CHARACTERS for a tactics game, drawn as game
sprites. These are things that crawled out of a poisonous bog.

CRITICAL BACKGROUND: the entire background, including all space between and around the four
creatures, must be 100% solid flat MAGENTA, hex #FF00FF. It is a chroma key and will be deleted.
No white, no grey, no gradient, no ground shadow, no drop shadow, no glow onto the background.
The creatures must not touch each other or the edges of the image. This instruction overrides
everything else in this prompt.

CRITICAL FORBIDDEN COLOURS: absolutely NO pink, NO magenta, NO violet, NO purple, NO lilac anywhere
on any creature. This matters more here than anywhere else — "poison" is often drawn purple, and
purple is FORBIDDEN because the background key colour is magenta and any purple would be destroyed
when the background is removed. Express poison with ACID YELLOW-GREEN and NEAR-BLACK instead. Use
only the specific colours named per cell below.

CAMERA: a 3/4 top-down view, as if looking down at a tabletop board from above and slightly in
front. Every creature faces the viewer, standing on flat invisible ground. All four must sit at the
SAME eye level and be seen from the SAME angle.

CRITICAL SIZE CONSTRAINT: these are displayed at only about 40 pixels tall in the game. Each
creature must be ONE bold chunky readable silhouette. NO fine detail, NO small parts, NO texture,
NO patterns, NO tiny highlights. If a feature would be smaller than a tenth of the creature's
width, delete it. These are low-tier trash mobs and should look SIMPLER than a hero character —
fewer shapes, fewer colours, one strong idea each.

STYLE — must match the game's heroes:
- Chunky, rounded, toy-like cartoon creatures. Slightly exaggerated, friendly-menacing, never gory
  and never scary.
- A THICK near-black outline (#1A1410) all the way around every creature and around its major
  internal shapes.
- FLAT colour fills. At most 3 colours per creature plus the outline, plus one lighter shade for a
  single simple cel-shaded highlight and one darker shade for a single shadow. No gradients, no soft
  shading, no gloss, no texture noise, no painterly brushwork, no pixel art.
- Big simple eyes where the creature has eyes at all. No noses, no ears, no separate fingers.

Per-cell contents:

TOP-LEFT — MUD HAND. A single enormous CLAWED HAND made of wet mud, pushing UP out of the ground —
there is no body, no head, no face, no eyes at all. It has exactly THREE thick stubby fingers, each
as fat as a tree trunk, curled slightly inward like a grasping claw. Do not draw five thin fingers.
The mud is a very dark NEAR-BLACK BROWN (#24211C) with one slightly lighter warm grey highlight on
the top of the knuckles. A low rounded mound of the same dark mud at the base where it meets the
ground. Overall the shape is compact, rounded and squat — wider than it is tall. This is the darkest
creature of the four. The idea "it is just a giant hand" is the whole silhouette.

TOP-RIGHT — BLOWPIPE BUG. A fat insect whose big round BULBOUS ABDOMEN sits high up and forms the
top of the silhouette, carried on four short thin legs below, so the shape is clearly TOP-HEAVY and
WIDE AT THE TOP, narrow at the bottom. From its small head at the front juts a thick BLOWPIPE that
FLARES OPEN at the far end like a short trumpet bell, pointing forward and slightly up. The pipe
must be chunky with real bulk, not a thin straw. Body is a bright ACID YELLOW-GREEN (#D2DC7A) with a
darker olive shadow under the abdomen, and the blowpipe is near-black. Two big simple round eyes.
This is the brightest creature of the four.

BOTTOM-LEFT — BOG CROCODILE. A four-legged crocodile standing low and long, seen from the front
three-quarter angle so all four legs and the body length are visible. Its LONG BLUNT SNOUT takes up
roughly HALF the total body length and juts straight forward — the absurdly long snout is the whole
silhouette idea. Back and flanks are a dark MOSS GREEN (#3D6B3D) with a few big chunky flat back
plates. The underside of the snout and the belly are a pale BONE CREAM, which must read clearly
against the dark green back. Two small simple eyes set high on the head. The silhouette should be
much WIDER than it is tall.

BOTTOM-RIGHT — MUD CRAB. A heavy crab whose enormous domed SHELL covers almost the whole creature,
reading as a walking dome. The shell is a pale desaturated GREY-GREEN (#A8B0A0) with a slightly
lighter rim and no more than four big chunky plate divisions — no fine mosaic. Four stubby legs at
the corners and two small simple eyes peeking out at the front under the shell. One single ASYMMETRIC
GIANT CLAW on one side only, clearly two or three times bigger than the other tiny claw — the
lopsided giant claw plus the dome is the memorable pairing. The dome must dominate the silhouette.

LAYOUT RULES:
1. EXACTLY 4 creatures in a clean 2x2 arrangement.
2. NO borders, NO dividing lines, NO frames, NO circles or platforms under the creatures.
3. Each creature fills about 70% of its cell with a clear magenta margin on all four sides. Nothing
   crosses into a neighbouring cell.
4. All four must feel like ONE set drawn by one artist: same outline thickness, same level of
   simplification, same camera angle.
5. NO text, no labels, no captions, no letters, no numbers, no watermark anywhere in the image.
