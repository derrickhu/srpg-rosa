# 骑兵 —— 四方向行走 4x4（generate2dsprite --mode player_sheet）
#
# 参考图：.pilot/ref_cavalry.png（从 lineup_v4_2 抠出的单帧，只锁身份+风格）
# 处理后 --write-scale-profile art/sprite-runs/cavalry/scale-profile.json，攻击 sheet 复用它。
#
# 骑乘单位是唯一「宽大于高」的兵种，这个剪影差异本身就是它的识别点，不要往人形去纠。
# 风格段落取自 _style_block.txt，行走段落改写自 _walk_motion_block.txt（人腿→马的步态）。

A 4x4 sprite sheet showing a full 4-direction walk cycle for a MOUNTED top-down tactics RPG unit.
Keep the character in the reference image exactly: same chibi lancer in warm golden-yellow armour
riding a small chunky WHITE horse with a cream mane, same thick near-black outlines, same flat
fills with light cel shading, same palette, same giant-head-tiny-body proportions for both rider
and horse. Only the facing direction and the walk pose change between cells.

HIS IDENTITY IS THE WHITE HORSE PLUS A LONG LANCE. The horse is small, chunky and cartoonish with
a big head and stubby legs, pure white with a cream mane — it is the light area of the unit and
must read as near-white. The rider holds a LONG LANCE with a broad silver head, carried at a clear
diagonal so it juts well past the horse's outline; the lance tip may rise above the rider's head.
Because he is mounted, his silhouette is the WIDEST and LOWEST of all the units — clearly a
different shape from a foot soldier.

WALK MOTION — the horse's gait must be readable from the SILHOUETTE ALONE at 44 pixels tall.
Columns 1 and 3 must look obviously different from columns 2 and 4 even with all interior detail
removed:
  Column 1: neutral. All four hooves gathered under the horse, legs straight, the whole unit at its
            FULL standing height.
  Column 2: the horse's near FORELEG reaches far FORWARD and its far HIND LEG stretches far BACK,
            with a clearly VISIBLE GAP between them — a wide, obvious stride, not a shuffle. The
            horse's body dips noticeably and the rider bounces down with it.
  Column 3: neutral again, identical to column 1.
  Column 4: the opposite legs swap — the other foreleg far forward, the other hind leg far back,
            same heavy dip.
Exaggerate far past realism. A subtle, realistic gait reads as a statue sliding across the board.

IDENTICAL BODY SCALE in all 16 cells: measure the unit from the top of the rider's helmet down to
the horse's hooves, ignoring the lance — that height must be the same in all 16 cells. Same camera
distance in every cell.

PROPORTIONS: extreme super-deformed mascot for both rider and horse. The rider's head including
helmet is about HALF of the rider's own height. The horse is stubby and barrel-shaped with short
thick legs, not a slender realistic horse. The whole unit is wider than it is tall.

SIMPLIFY: only 4 to 6 flat colours in total. WARM GOLDEN YELLOW is the rider's identity colour; the
white horse is the light area. No belts, straps, buckles, reins detail, saddle stitching, rivets,
armour seams or trim. Face is minimal: two simple dark eyes, two eyebrows, at most a tiny mouth
line. Arms and legs are simple rounded stubs; hands are mitten shapes with no fingers.

ART STYLE: hand-drawn cartoon game sprite, thick near-black outlines, flat saturated fills with
light cel shading (one highlight tone, one shadow tone), no gradients, no texture noise, no
painterly rendering. Bright saturated palette with a genuine near-white highlight.

CAMERA: 3/4 top-down view from slightly above, consistent in all 16 cells.

SHEET LAYOUT — rows are facing direction:
  Row 1 (top):    facing DOWN toward the camera — you see the horse's face and the rider's face
  Row 2:          facing LEFT, full left side profile of horse and rider
  Row 3:          facing RIGHT, full right side profile, mirrored from row 2
  Row 4 (bottom): facing UP away from the camera — you see the horse's rump and tail and the
                  rider's back. The lance must still be plainly visible.

ABSOLUTE RULES:
1. EXACTLY 16 equal-size cells in a 4x4 grid, every cell the same width and height.
2. NO borders, NO dividing lines, NO frames between cells — cells separated only by flat
   background colour.
3. Background is 100% solid flat pure magenta #FF00FF everywhere including between cells.
   No gradient, no vignette, no shadow.
4. IDENTICAL identity, costume and palette in all 16 cells.
5. The UNIT (rider's helmet down to the hooves, lance excluded) fills about 45% of the cell height
   in EVERY cell. The lance extends past that.
6. Nothing crosses a cell edge. The lance tip especially must stay clear of the edges.
7. Hooves rest on the same horizontal baseline in every cell — the body dips by squashing, not by
   floating.
8. NO text, NO labels, NO numbers, NO arrows, NO UI, NO watermark anywhere.
