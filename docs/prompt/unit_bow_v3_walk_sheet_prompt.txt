A 4x4 sprite sheet showing a full 4-direction walk cycle for a top-down tactics RPG unit.
Keep the character in the reference image exactly: same face, same costume, same palette,
same bow. Only the facing direction and the walk pose change between cells.

CHARACTER: a cheerful young forest archer.
- LEAF GREEN is his identity colour and covers most of his body, including a simple pointed
  hood. A plain cream / off-white short cape is his one light area — it must read as
  near-white (#F0F0E0 / #E0E0D0), not beige and not grey.
- HIS PROP IS A BIG TALL BOW and it is his whole identity. Requirements, all mandatory:
  * It is TALLER THAN HE IS. The upper tip rises clearly ABOVE THE TOP OF HIS HOOD and the
    lower tip reaches down past his knees. Drawing the bow small is the main way to fail here.
  * Held VERTICALLY at his side, close to the body, fully in view, never hidden behind him.
  * Thick honey-wood limbs with a clear double curve, plus a STRAIGHT PALE BOWSTRING running
    between the two tips, so the whole thing reads as an unmistakable D shape at tiny size.
    Without the string it looks like a horn or a croissant, which is wrong.
  * A bow that rises above his head is REQUIRED, not merely allowed. In game the character is
    scaled by his body height only, so a tall bow costs him nothing.
- Warm reddish-brown hair showing under the hood, warm tan boots.
- No quiver, no arrows, no feathers, no belt, no pouch.

PROPORTIONS — check against this, drawing him too tall is the most common failure:
- Extreme super-deformed mascot. His HEAD INCLUDING HAIR AND HOOD is about HALF of his total
  body height. Body, arms and legs together are the other half: short, stubby, chunky,
  almost no neck.
- His BODY (top of the head down to the boots, not counting the bow) fits in a roughly
  SQUARE box — about as wide as it is tall. If he reads as a slim standing person, it is wrong.
- This is NOT a 2-head or 2.5-head chibi. Think 1.5-head mascot: giant head, tiny body.

SIMPLIFY — characterise him with a few bold strokes and colours, never by piling on detail.
He displays about 48 pixels tall in game, where detail becomes noise:
- Only 4 to 6 flat colours on the whole character.
- NO belts, straps, buckles, pouches, rivets, seams, laces, stitching, patterns or trim.
- Face is minimal: two simple dark eyes, two eyebrows, at most a tiny mouth line. No nose,
  no ear detail, no blush.
- Arms and legs are simple rounded stubs; hands are mitten shapes with no fingers.

ART STYLE (project style bible — do not deviate):
hand-drawn cartoon game sprite, thick near-black outlines (pure black or very dark warm
brown — never a mid-tone or colorful outline), flat saturated fills with light cel shading
(one highlight tone, one shadow tone), no gradients, no texture noise, no painterly
rendering, no photographic detail.

BRIGHTNESS: bright saturated palette with a genuine near-white highlight. Never uniformly
dark or muted. Avoid grey, dusty, chalky or desaturated colours. Avoid olive-drab and khaki.

CAMERA: 3/4 top-down view from slightly above, consistent in all 16 cells. You can see the
top of the head, the shoulders, and the full body down to the boots.

SHEET LAYOUT — rows are facing direction, columns are walk frames:
  Row 1 (top):    facing DOWN, toward the camera, face fully visible
  Row 2:          facing LEFT, left side profile
  Row 3:          facing RIGHT, right side profile, mirrored from row 2
  Row 4 (bottom): facing UP, away from the camera, back of the hood and cape visible.
                  The tall bow must still be clearly visible at his side in this row —
                  a back view that is just a green hood and a cream cape has no identity.

ABSOLUTE RULES:
1. EXACTLY 16 equal-size cells in a 4x4 grid. Every cell the same width and height.
2. NO borders, NO dividing lines, NO frames between cells. Cells are separated only by the
   flat background color.
3. Background is 100% solid flat magenta #FF00FF everywhere, including between the cells.
   No gradient, no vignette, no ground shadow, no drop shadow under the character.
4. IDENTICAL SCALE in all 16 cells: same head-to-boot height, same shoulder width, same
   camera distance. Do not zoom in or out between cells. Do not crop tighter in some cells.
5. IDENTICAL character identity, costume, and palette in all 16 cells.
6. His BODY (head to boots) fills about 45% of the cell height; the tall bow makes the whole
   thing about 65%. Centered in its cell with even magenta margin on all four sides.
   Do NOT satisfy this by shrinking the bow — shrink the character instead.
7. Nothing crosses a cell edge — no hair, cape, bow, boot or hand may touch or overlap a
   neighboring cell. The bow tips especially must stay well clear of the top and bottom
   edges. If in doubt, draw the character smaller.
8. Feet rest on the same horizontal baseline in every cell.
9. NO text, NO labels, NO numbers, NO arrows, NO UI, NO watermark anywhere in the image.
