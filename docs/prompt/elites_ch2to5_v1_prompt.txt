# 第二至五章精英 —— 四只一张 2x2（generate2dsprite --mode grid --rows 2 --cols 2）
#
# 设计依据：docs/敌人图鉴.md §2 各章「精英」行。第一章精英百夫长·卡格**刻意沿用 bloodfang**
# （他是酋长的部下，提前认脸），所以这里只做第二到五章的四只。
#
# ## 为什么四只一批
#
# 一次出全套，四只的描边粗细、简化程度、俯视角度天然一致；分四次生图做不到。
# 而精英之间虽然不同章不同场，但玩家会在**图鉴/单位面板**里横向比较，风格漂移看得出来。
#
# ## 为什么精英统一是人形兽人
#
# 血牙部族是贯穿五章的敌对势力：**杂兵是当地野物，精英与 Boss 是血牙部族**。
# 这条读图规矩第一章就立下了，剪影本身就是「这个不好惹」的信号。
# 于是精英在第二、四、五章天然和本章杂兵分开（人形 vs 非人形），
# 只有第三章需要认真算——那一章杂兵也是人形兽人，靠饰色（金）+ 单侧巨大肩甲区分。
#
# 例外是第五章的龙裔·卡尔萨：他是**半龙化**的兽人，仍是人形，但一侧手臂已变成龙爪。
#
# ## 配色推演（判据：色相差 ≥25° 或明度差 ≥20，满足一条即可）
#
#   1 猎长·图伦（第 2 章）  深皮革棕 L30 + 血红 + 骨白长弓
#     本章杂兵明度是 70/85/50/40，他取**最暗**的 30 立住。血红对林影豹的锈橙
#     只差 10° 色相、5 明度，单看配色是不够的——所以**主体读法压在骨白长弓上**：
#     一条比人还高的竖线，和四足低伏的豹在 40px 下是两个完全不同的形。
#
#   2 血牙城卫长（第 3 章）绿皮 + 暗铁 + **金**
#     全章唯一的金。这一章统一色是绿皮 + 暗铁，三只杂兵饰色分别是血红/骨白/血红，
#     金是第四个，不撞。金对我方骑兵 H42 L52 要注意：骑兵是白马 + 长枪的横向剪影，
#     这只是直立军官，跨剪影类别。
#
#   3 沼语者·塔玛（第 4 章）近黑袍 + **酸黄绿**吊壶
#     酸黄绿是本章的签名色（吹箭虫同色系）。他和吹箭虫的区别不靠颜色靠形：
#     人形直立 vs 圆腹昆虫。近黑袍对泥沼手 L12 同样靠形分开。
#
#   4 龙裔·卡尔萨（第 5 章）绿皮 + 黑曜鳞 + 橙红龙爪
#     **绿皮是他的识别点**：本章四只杂兵没有一只是绿的（黑曜/橙红/灰黑/苍白）。
#     橙红只用在那只龙爪上，和本章签名色呼应但不夺主。
#
# ## 剪影必须互不相同（这是四只放一张图最容易翻的地方）
#
#   图伦   = 高瘦 + 一条比人还高的**竖线**（长弓）
#   城卫长 = 上宽 + **肩部**单侧巨大块（肩甲）
#   塔玛   = 弓背下沉 + **垂下来**的一个小块（吊壶）
#   卡尔萨 = **手部**单侧巨大块（龙爪），位置刻意和城卫长的肩部错开
#
# 城卫长和卡尔萨都是「单侧巨大块」，靠**块在肩还是在手**区分，配色再帮一把。
#
# 禁色：粉、品红、紫罗兰一律不许出现（抠色键是品红 #FF00FF）。
#
# **不要写「某个形状必须支配剪影」**——那条最高级指令会把另一个识别特征直接删掉，
# 第三章闸门盾卫和第四章泥壳蟹连着栽过两次，见 docs/美术管线-AI-sprite.md §2.9。
# 要主次就描述比例。

A 2-column x 2-row grid of 4 CARTOON ORC ELITE CHARACTERS for a top-down tactics game, drawn as game
sprites. All four belong to the same Bloodfang orc tribe and must read as one faction.

CRITICAL BACKGROUND: the entire background, including all space between and around the four
characters, must be 100% solid flat MAGENTA, hex #FF00FF. It is a chroma key and will be deleted.
No white, no grey, no gradient, no ground shadow, no drop shadow, no glow onto the background.
The characters must not touch each other or the edges of the image. This instruction overrides
everything else in this prompt.

CRITICAL FORBIDDEN COLOURS: absolutely NO pink, NO magenta, NO violet, NO purple, NO lilac anywhere
on any character. The background key colour is magenta, so any pinkish-purple on a character will be
destroyed when the background is removed.

CAMERA: a 3/4 top-down view, as if looking down at a tabletop board from above and slightly in
front. Every character faces the viewer, standing on flat invisible ground. All four must sit at the
SAME eye level, be seen from the SAME angle, and have the SAME head-to-boot height.

CRITICAL SIZE CONSTRAINT: these are displayed at only about 40 pixels tall in the game. Each
character must be ONE bold chunky readable silhouette. NO fine detail, NO small parts, NO texture,
NO patterns, NO tiny highlights. If a feature would be smaller than a tenth of the character's
width, delete it. These are mid-tier elites: a little more ornate than a trash mob, but still much
simpler than a hero.

STYLE:
- Chunky, rounded, toy-like cartoon characters with chibi proportions — large head, short body.
  Slightly exaggerated, friendly-menacing, never gory and never scary.
- A THICK near-black outline (#1A1410) all the way around every character and around its major
  internal shapes.
- FLAT colour fills only. At most 4 colours per character plus the outline, plus one lighter shade
  for a single simple cel-shaded highlight and one darker shade for a single shadow. No gradients,
  no soft shading, no gloss, no shiny spheres, no texture noise, no painterly brushwork, no pixel art.
- Big simple eyes. No noses, no ears, no fingers, no teeth smaller than the outline width.
- Shared tribal identity: ash-grey-green orc skin, two small tusks in the lower jaw, and somewhere on
  each one a small bone ornament.

Per-cell contents:

TOP-LEFT — HUNT MASTER. A lean orc hunter standing upright, holding a HUGE LONGBOW VERTICALLY beside
him. The bow is BONE WHITE (#E8E4D8) and is TALLER THAN HE IS, reaching from the ground to above the
top of his head — this tall pale vertical line beside a dark body is his whole read. His clothing is
DARK LEATHER BROWN (#4A3A2A), the darkest body of the four. Blood red (#B23A32) war paint stripes
across one cheek and a blood red cloth band on one arm. A short quiver of stubby bolts at his hip.
He is the TALLEST and NARROWEST of the four.

TOP-RIGHT — CASTELLAN. A broad orc officer standing square to the viewer in DARK IRON (#2E3238) plate
with green skin showing at the arms and face. On ONE SHOULDER ONLY sits an ENORMOUS ANGULAR PAULDRON
about as wide as his own torso, painted BRASS GOLD (#B8863A) with a lighter gold rim — the lopsided
shoulder is his read. He has ONE EYE: the other is covered by a plain dark iron eye patch, drawn as
one simple flat shape. He holds a short broad blade low in the other hand. He is the WIDEST ACROSS
THE SHOULDERS of the four, and the asymmetric mass sits HIGH, at shoulder level.

BOTTOM-LEFT — MIRE SPEAKER. A hunched orc witch doctor with a rounded curved back, leaning forward
over a crooked bone staff. HANGING DOWN from the top of the staff on a short cord is a fat round
BONE FLASK, dangling clearly below his hand at about waist height, with a few flat drip shapes
falling from its bottom — the dangling flask under a bent-over body is his read. The flask's contents
show as ACID YELLOW-GREEN (#D2DC7A) painted as a hard-edged flat shape, no glow. His robe is NEAR
BLACK (#24211C). He is the SHORTEST and roundest of the four.

BOTTOM-RIGHT — DRAKE KIN. An orc warrior mid-transformation into a dragon. His body and legs are
covered in flat OBSIDIAN BLACK (#1F1D22) scale plates, and his green orc skin is still clearly
visible on his face and one normal arm. His OTHER ARM has become an ENORMOUS DRAGON CLAW, about twice
the thickness of his normal arm, held DOWN AND FORWARD at hip height with three big blunt talons.
The claw is a deep ORANGE RED (#C64A28). Two short blunt horns on his head. The asymmetric mass sits
LOW, at hand level — this must not read like a shoulder pad.

LAYOUT RULES:
1. EXACTLY 4 characters in a clean 2x2 arrangement.
2. NO borders, NO dividing lines, NO frames, NO circles or platforms under the characters.
3. Each character fills about 70% of its cell with a clear magenta margin on all four sides. Nothing
   crosses into a neighbouring cell — not a bow tip, not a pauldron, not a talon.
4. All four must feel like ONE set drawn by one artist and like ONE tribe: same outline thickness,
   same skin tone, same level of simplification, same camera angle, same body height.
5. NO text, no labels, no captions, no letters, no numbers, no watermark anywhere in the image.
