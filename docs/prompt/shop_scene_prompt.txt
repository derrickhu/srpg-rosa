局内补给商店场景道具（神秘商人 + 木摊）
产出 → images/ui/shop_merchant.png、images/ui/shop_stall.png
生成器：Cursor 内置 GenerateImage（可分两次出，保证各自构图）

切图：
    python3 scripts/split-tile-sheet.py --input <raw>.png --out-dir .tmp-gen/shop_scene \
        --names shop_merchant --size 288 --grid 1x1 --min-area 200
    # 或 stall：--names shop_stall --size 400
    python3 scripts/despill-magenta.py .tmp-gen/shop_scene
    # 再拷到 images/ui/

用在哪：局内补给点 ShopView 场景层。商人站摊后，摊台摆 3 件货（货与价签由代码叠）。
显示约：商人高 140–180px，摊宽约屏幕 85%。

主色：商人斗篷深靛 `#3A4A6C`（与 UI panel 冷蓝灰同族，避开四职业色相）；
货袋暖褐 `#8A5A30`；摊木 `#A87840` / 台布米白 `#FCFCF6` 或草绿条。
记忆点：深兜帽遮脸 + 鼓鼓货袋（神秘商人）。

约束：
- 品红键色 #FF00FF 底，透明导出
- 厚近黑描边 #1A1410，平涂，无渐变/发光/文字/价签/UI 框
- 不要画摊上的商品（药/书/券）——运行时叠图标

---

ART STYLE (project style bible — do not deviate):
hand-drawn cartoon game sprite, chibi / super-deformed proportions with a large head
and short limbs, thick near-black outlines (pure black or very dark warm brown — never
a mid-tone or colorful outline), flat saturated fills with light cel shading (one
highlight tone, one shadow tone), no gradients, no texture noise, no painterly
rendering, no photographic detail. Readable silhouette. NO TEXT, no labels, no captions,
no watermark anywhere in the image.

---

### A) shop_merchant (GenerateImage aspect 3:4)

ONE character sprite on solid flat MAGENTA #FF00FF background.
Mysterious chibi merchant for a tactics roguelike supply stop:
- Large hooded head, face hidden in deep hood shadow (tiny soft eye glints optional)
- Short chibi body, deep indigo cloak #3A4A6C with one cel highlight
- One fat bulging brown travel sack #8A5A30 slung at the side (THE signature feature)
- Thick near-black outline #1A1410, flat fills, chunky toy-like
- Standing 3/4 view facing slightly toward camera-right
- Clear magenta margin; NO stall, NO goods, NO price tags, NO text, NO circular badge

### B) shop_stall (GenerateImage aspect 16:9)

ONE prop sprite on solid flat MAGENTA #FF00FF background.
Low wooden merchant stall / roadside table for the same game:
- Wide short counter, chunky planks #A87840, thick near-black outline
- Simple cloth runner on top in cream #FCFCF6 with one thin green stripe
- Empty tabletop — leave clear space for 3 items to be placed in code
- Optional two short front legs; no shelves of goods, no bottles, no books
- Flat cel shading, no gradients, no text, no price tags, no UI frames
- Clear magenta margin all around
