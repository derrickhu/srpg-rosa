- Puffy rounded cartoon boulders in the SAME blob language as the reference bushes — medium warm tan #D4B080 with cream sunlit tops. Soft and toy-like.
- NOT jagged black rocks, NOT obsidian, NOT thick black outlines.
- Soft outlines in a slightly deeper tan. Optional: at most two tiny bright orange specks tucked in the FRAME only, never in the centre, never a lava flow.

ART STYLE: hand-drawn cartoon, flat saturated fills, simple cel shading, clean and cheerful. Low frequency.
