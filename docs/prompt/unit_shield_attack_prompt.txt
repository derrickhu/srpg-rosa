CRITICAL BACKGROUND REQUIREMENT, THIS OVERRIDES EVERYTHING ELSE: the background must be 100% solid
flat MAGENTA, hex #FF00FF. NOT black, NOT white, NOT grey, NOT a gradient. Every pixel that is not
the character must be exactly magenta #FF00FF, including between the cells. It is a chroma key.

The attached reference image contains ONE SINGLE character frame. It defines only the CHARACTER
IDENTITY and the ART STYLE, NOT the layout. Do NOT copy the reference's framing and do NOT produce
a walk cycle — produce the 2x2 attack sheet described below.

Match the reference exactly on: the same stout chibi shield bearer, same steel-blue armour and blue
hood, same cream off-white rectangular tower shield with a bold blue cross, same thick near-black
outlines, same flat fills with light cel shading, same giant-head-tiny-body proportions.

A 2x2 sprite sheet: 4 frames of a SHIELD BASH attack. <朝向段落>. He rams forward with the tower
shield — this is his signature move, so the shield must be the thing that moves, and the change
between frames must be obvious in silhouette.

  朝下：the character FACING DOWN toward the camera the whole time
  朝左：the character seen in LEFT SIDE PROFILE, facing LEFT the whole time and ramming toward the
        left. He stays in LEFT profile in all 4 frames — never turn him toward the camera, never
        flip him to face right.
  朝上：the character seen FROM BEHIND, facing UP and AWAY from the camera the whole time, ramming
        away from the viewer. His face is never visible. Keep at least part of the cream shield
        face visible past his silhouette in every frame so the unit stays recognisable.

FRAME ORDER, left-to-right then top-to-bottom:
  Frame 1 (top-left):     WIND-UP. He pulls the shield back tight against his shoulder, knees bent,
                          body coiled.
  Frame 2 (top-right):    DRIVE. He lunges forward and thrusts the shield out ahead of him, leading
                          foot stepping forward.
  Frame 3 (bottom-left):  IMPACT. Full extension — the shield is rammed as far forward as it goes,
                          arm straight, leading foot planted wide, body lunged behind it. Strongest,
                          most extreme pose. He STAYS UPRIGHT — do not bend him double, do not hide
                          his head.
  Frame 4 (bottom-right): RECOVERY. He pulls the shield back to a neutral guard at his side, body
                          straightening.

ABSOLUTE RULES:
1. Background is 100% solid flat magenta #FF00FF everywhere including between cells. No gradient,
   no vignette, no shadow.
2. EXACTLY 4 equal-size cells in a 2x2 grid, every cell the same width and height.
3. NO borders, NO dividing lines, NO gutters, NO frames between cells.
4. IDENTICAL BODY SCALE and IDENTICAL camera distance in all 4 cells. Measure him from the top of
   his head to the soles of his boots, ignoring the shield: that height must be the same in all 4
   cells. Do not zoom in on the action.
5. HIS BODY (head-top to boots, shield excluded) FILLS ABOUT 55% OF THE CELL HEIGHT. Draw him
   generously big in the cell — a tiny figure lost in a huge empty cell is WRONG.
6. Nothing crosses a cell edge.
7. Feet rest on the same horizontal baseline in every cell.
8. Draw the CHARACTER BODY ONLY. NO motion-blur streaks, NO impact flash, NO dust, NO sparks,
   NO shockwave, NO speed lines.
9. NO text, no labels, no numbers, no arrows, no UI, no watermark.
