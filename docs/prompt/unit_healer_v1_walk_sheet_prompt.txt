# 单位 healer v1（祭司职业 · 弥尔）—— 四方向行走 sheet（generate2dsprite player_sheet, 4x4）
#
# 取代 healer/idle 单帧捷径。风格、比例、键色对齐剑士/弓手 v3 / 法师 v1。
#
# 定色（美术风格圣经 §2.1）：
#   主色 CREAM `#F5E6C8` H40 L90。对骑兵金 H42 色相几乎重叠，靠明度差 ≥20 和「人形 + 太阳杖」
#   剪影分开（骑兵是金 + 白马）。薄荷绿腰带是点缀，不是主色，避免和法师青绿袍、弓手叶绿撞。
#   禁止紫/粉/品红。禁止绿斗篷（弓手）、钢蓝甲（盾卫）、尖帽（法师）。
#
# 剪影一句话：乳白近正方形短肢身体，身旁一颗头那么大的儿童涂鸦太阳。
# 唯一记忆点：那颗失真的大金太阳（圆盘 + 6 根粗三角芒），不是袍子上的花纹。
#
# 键色：#FF00FF —— 乳白/金/棕，与品红欧氏距离远。
# 下游：generate2dsprite.py process --target player --mode player_sheet --align feet
#       --shared-scale --component-mode largest --write-scale-profile
# 参考图：art/sprite-runs/healer/identity-ref-down.png

A 4x4 sprite sheet showing a full 4-direction walk cycle for a top-down tactics RPG unit.
Keep the character in the reference image: same face, same cream-white priest robe, same gold
collar band, same mint-green sash, same warm brown hair, same huge gold sun-disc staff, same
palette. Only the facing direction, the walk pose and how the staff sits change.

HIS SUN STAFF IS HIS WHOLE IDENTITY — it must be huge.
All of the following are mandatory:
- A FAT GOLD SUN DISC on a thick brown wooden staff. The disc is a SOLID circle about as big as
  his head, with SIX THICK TRIANGULAR RAYS like a child's drawing of the sun. At tiny size the
  circle-plus-rays is the only way to read "priest" — a tiny star on a stick turns him into a
  generic child with a twig.
- The sun disc rising well above his head is REQUIRED, not merely allowed. In game the character
  is scaled by his body height only (staff excluded from bodySpan), so a huge sun costs him nothing.
- Do not shrink the sun to fit the cell. Shrink the character instead.
- Hold the staff close to the body so it does not widen the bbox more than needed.

PROPORTIONS — check against this, drawing him too tall is the most common failure:
- Extreme super-deformed mascot. His HEAD INCLUDING HAIR is about HALF of his body height.
  Body, arms and legs together are the other half: short, stubby, chunky, almost no neck.
- His BODY, from the top of his hair down to his boots and not counting the staff, fits in a
  roughly SQUARE box — about as wide as it is tall.
- This is NOT a 2-head or 2.5-head chibi. Think 1.5-head mascot: giant head, tiny body.
- He is a toy-soldier child, NOT a pretty teen, NOT a slim anime boy.

CHARACTER: a young boy priest.
- CREAM-WHITE `#F5E6C8` is his identity colour and covers most of his robe.
- A thick GOLD `#E0B030` collar band is his one metal accent.
- A simple MINT-GREEN `#5EC8A0` sash across the middle — one flat stripe, no knot, no trim.
- Simple warm brown hair, short and neat, warm tan boots, skin #EEC49A.
- No cape, no hood, no hat, no belt buckle, no embroidery, no tiny crosses, no writing.

SIMPLIFY — characterise him with a few bold strokes and colours, never by piling on detail.
He displays about 48 pixels tall in game, where detail becomes noise:
- Only 4 to 6 flat colours: cream, gold, mint sash, skin, warm brown, near-black outline.
- NO belts, straps, buckles, pouches, rivets, seams, laces, stitching, patterns or trim.
- Face is minimal: two simple dark eyes, two eyebrows, at most a tiny mouth line. No nose,
  no ear detail, no blush, no eyelashes.
- Arms and legs are simple rounded stubs; hands are mitten shapes with no fingers.
- NO purple, magenta, pink, or violet anywhere on the character.
- NO green hood, NO blue armour, NO pointed wizard hat.

ART STYLE (project style bible — do not deviate):
hand-drawn cartoon game sprite, chibi / super-deformed proportions with a large head
and short limbs, thick near-black outlines (pure black or very dark warm brown — never
a mid-tone or colorful outline), flat saturated fills with light cel shading (one
highlight tone, one shadow tone), no gradients, no texture noise, no painterly
rendering, no photographic detail, 3/4 top-down camera matching a tactics grid.
The sprite must stay readable at 40 pixels tall: silhouette first, exaggerate the
weapon and the one signature feature, drop everything that needs zoom to appreciate.
NO TEXT, no labels, no captions, no watermark anywhere in the image.

BRIGHTNESS: bright saturated palette with a genuine near-white cream robe. Never uniformly
dark or muted. Avoid grey, dusty, chalky or desaturated colours.

CAMERA: 3/4 top-down view from slightly above, consistent in all 16 cells. You can see the
top of the head, the shoulders, and the full body down to the boots.

SHEET LAYOUT — rows are facing direction, columns are walk frames:
  Row 1 (top):    facing DOWN, toward the camera, face fully visible
  Row 2:          facing LEFT, left side profile
  Row 3:          facing RIGHT, right side profile, mirrored from row 2
  Row 4 (bottom): facing UP, away from the camera, back of the head and robe visible.
                  The huge gold sun disc must still be plainly visible in this row —
                  a back view that is just a cream blob has no identity.
WALK MOTION — the walk must be readable from the SILHOUETTE ALONE at 44 pixels tall.
Columns 1 and 3 must look obviously different from columns 2 and 4 even with all interior
detail removed. Understating this is the single most common failure:
  Column 1: neutral. Both feet together directly under the body, legs straight, body at its
            FULL standing height.
  Column 2: LEFT foot planted far FORWARD, right foot trailing far BEHIND, with a clearly
            VISIBLE GAP between the two feet — the stride is wide, not a shuffle. The whole
            body drops noticeably, squashing down about one tenth of its height. Arms swing
            opposite to the legs.
  Column 3: neutral again, identical to column 1.
  Column 4: mirror of column 2 — RIGHT foot far forward, left foot trailing behind, same
            heavy body drop, arms swung the other way.
Exaggerate far past realism. A subtle, realistic walk reads as a statue sliding across the
board. If in doubt, make the stride wider and the body drop deeper.

ABSOLUTE RULES:
1. EXACTLY 16 equal-size cells in a 4x4 grid. Every cell the same width and height.
2. NO borders, NO dividing lines, NO frames between cells. Cells are separated only by the
   flat background color.
3. Background is 100% solid flat magenta #FF00FF everywhere, including between the cells.
   No gradient, no vignette, no ground shadow, no drop shadow under the character.
4. IDENTICAL BODY SCALE in all 16 cells. Measure him from the top of his hair to the soles of
   his boots, IGNORING the staff: that height must be the same in all 16 cells. Do not shrink
   the body in the cells where the sun happens to take up more room.
5. IDENTICAL character identity, costume, and palette in all 16 cells.
6. His BODY (hair-top to boots, staff excluded) fills about 45% of the cell height in EVERY
   cell. Centered with magenta margin on all four sides.
7. Nothing crosses a cell edge. The sun rays especially must stay clear of the edges.
8. Feet rest on the same horizontal baseline in every cell.
9. NO text, NO labels, NO numbers, NO arrows, NO UI, NO watermark anywhere in the image.
