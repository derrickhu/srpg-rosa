# attack_right 由 attack_left 镜像，不出图。下面 <朝向段落> 三选一。

CRITICAL BACKGROUND REQUIREMENT: the background must be 100% solid flat MAGENTA, hex #FF00FF.
NOT black, NOT white, NOT grey, NOT a gradient. Every pixel that is not the character or horse must
be exactly magenta #FF00FF, including between the cells. It is a chroma key.

The attached reference image contains ONE SINGLE frame. It defines only the CHARACTER IDENTITY and
the ART STYLE, NOT the layout. Do NOT copy the reference's framing and do NOT produce a walk cycle
— produce the 2x2 attack sheet described below.

Match the reference exactly on: the same chibi lancer in warm golden-yellow armour riding a small
chunky WHITE horse with a cream mane, same long lance with a broad silver head, same thick
near-black outlines, same flat fills with light cel shading, same giant-head-tiny-body proportions
for both rider and horse.

A 2x2 sprite sheet: 4 frames of a LANCE CHARGE. <朝向段落>. The lance thrust is the whole point —
it must be the thing that moves.

  朝下：horse and rider FACING DOWN toward the camera the whole time, attacking toward the viewer.
        You see the horse's face and the rider's face in every frame. The rider holds the lance out
        to one side at a steep diagonal, tip angled down toward the viewer, so it stays clearly
        visible instead of vanishing into foreshortening.
  朝左：horse and rider seen in LEFT SIDE PROFILE, facing LEFT the whole time and attacking toward
        the left. They stay in LEFT profile in all 4 frames — never turn them toward the camera,
        never flip them to face right.
  朝上：horse and rider seen FROM BEHIND, facing UP and AWAY from the camera the whole time. You see
        the horse's rump and tail and the rider's back; their faces are never visible. The rider
        holds the lance out to one side at a steep diagonal so it stays clearly visible.

FRAME ORDER, left-to-right then top-to-bottom:
  Frame 1 (top-left):     WIND-UP. The rider cocks the lance back and up, the horse gathering with
                          its legs tucked.
  Frame 2 (top-right):    CHARGE. The horse surges forward in a big stride, the lance levelling and
                          driving forward.
  Frame 3 (bottom-left):  IMPACT. Full extension — the lance is thrust as far forward as it goes,
                          the horse at full stretch with forelegs far out and hind legs far back,
                          rider leaning hard into it. Strongest, most extreme pose.
  Frame 4 (bottom-right): RECOVERY. The lance is drawn back up to a raised diagonal carry, the horse
                          settling back onto all four hooves.

ABSOLUTE RULES:
1. Background is 100% solid flat magenta #FF00FF everywhere including between cells. No gradient,
   no vignette, no shadow.
2. EXACTLY 4 equal-size cells in a 2x2 grid, every cell the same width and height.
3. NO borders, NO dividing lines, NO gutters, NO frames between cells.
4. IDENTICAL BODY SCALE and IDENTICAL camera distance in all 4 cells. Measure the unit from the top
   of the rider's helmet down to the hooves, ignoring the lance: that height must be the same in all
   4 cells. Do not zoom in on the action.
5. THE UNIT (helmet down to hooves, lance excluded) FILLS ABOUT 55% OF THE CELL HEIGHT. Draw it
   generously big in the cell — a tiny figure lost in a huge empty cell is WRONG. The lance extends
   past that and may come fairly close to the cell edge.
6. Nothing crosses a cell edge. The lance tip especially must stay clear of the edges.
7. Hooves rest on the same horizontal baseline in every cell.
8. Draw the RIDER AND HORSE ONLY. NO motion-blur streaks, NO impact flash, NO dust, NO sparks,
   NO speed lines.
9. NO text, no labels, no numbers, no arrows, no UI, no watermark.
