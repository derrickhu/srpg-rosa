One single terrain STICKER, centered, for a tactics grid cell.

The attached reference shows the sticker language: a small centered prop with a soft cream
rim and a soft contact shadow, sitting alone. Match that sticker construction. The subject
is a fog puff.

CRITICAL BACKGROUND: 100% solid flat MAGENTA #FF00FF. Chroma key. The sticker floats in
the middle with a wide magenta margin. No tile, no grass, no grid.

SUBJECT: one chubby cloud puff of rainy-window gray-blue. Main body #C5D4DE, a slightly
deeper shadow lobe #8AA0B0, a steamed-bun cream highlight #F0F0E0 on the upper lobe.
Soft rounded edge, a thin cream rim so it reads on green grass. A very soft darker contact
shadow tucked under the puff. No black outline. No face. No eyes. Flat cartoon, 3 tones,
soft edges, toy-like volume like the reference pool sticker.

The puff fills about 55% of the square, centered. NO text, no icon frame, no characters.
