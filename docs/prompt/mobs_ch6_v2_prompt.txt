A 2-column x 2-row grid of 4 CARTOON MONSTER CHARACTERS for a tactics game, drawn as game sprites.
Keep the same cute identities as the reference portraits, but EVERY creature must sit in the
CENTER 60–68% of its cell with a thick MAGENTA margin. Nothing may touch a cell edge.

CRITICAL BACKGROUND: the entire background, including all space between and around the four
creatures, must be 100% solid flat MAGENTA, hex #FF00FF. It is a chroma key and will be deleted.
No white, no grey, no gradient, no ground shadow, no drop shadow.

CAMERA: a 3/4 top-down view. Every creature faces the viewer. Same eye level, same angle.

CRITICAL SIZE CONSTRAINT: displayed at only about 40 pixels tall. ONE bold chunky readable
silhouette each. NO fine detail. Low-tier trash mobs, simpler than a hero.

CRITICAL CONTAINMENT: each creature INCLUDING wings, horns, tails, and feet must stay inside
the central 60–68% of its own cell. Leave a wide magenta gutter. Do NOT spread wings to the
cell walls. If a wing or horn would reach the edge, fold it shorter and finish the tip.

STYLE: chunky rounded toy-like cartoon. THICK near-black outline (#1A1410) all the way around
every silhouette, including wing tips. FLAT saturated fills, at most 3 colours plus outline
plus one highlight and one shadow. No gradients, no gore, no purple, no pink, no pixel art.
Big simple eyes.

Per-cell contents (match the attached reference portraits, but SMALLER in the cell):

TOP-LEFT — BONE PUPPET (sword). A fat rounded teardrop of pale BONE-IVORY (#E8E0D4) sitting
on the ground with no legs. A few dry dark-red spots (#7A2020). Two big round black eyes.
Keep the cute blob. Do not fill the cell.

TOP-RIGHT — GORE CROW (bow). The same cute white owl as the reference: round head, black

Add a tiny dry-red (#7A2020) beak or chest mark so it is not pure white. Thick outline on
every wing tip.

BOTTOM-LEFT — RITE HORN BEAST (cavalry). The same cute white four-legged lizard as the
reference, two short forward horns. Keep it low and wide, but shrink it so horns, nose, and

BOTTOM-RIGHT — SLAB WARDEN (shield). The same heavy limestone dome walker as the reference.
Cold limestone (#8A8578 / #C4C0B8). NO blood red. Four stubby feet fully visible. Dome does
not touch the cell edges.

LAYOUT RULES:
1. EXACTLY 4 creatures in a clean 2x2.
2. NO borders, NO frames, NO platforms.
