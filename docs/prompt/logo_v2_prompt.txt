A game title logo for a cartoon fantasy tactics mobile game. The logo consists of exactly FOUR Chinese characters written horizontally in a single row, reading left to right:

无 尽 纹 章

These four characters, and nothing else, must appear. Write them accurately and legibly as proper Chinese characters with correct stroke structure. Do not invent extra strokes, do not add any other characters, do not add any Latin letters, numbers, or Japanese kana.

TYPOGRAPHY: heavy, bold, chunky display lettering with wide strokes — the kind of thick brush-like title face used on casual mobile game splash screens. The four characters are evenly spaced, all the same size, sitting on one straight horizontal baseline.

COLOUR AND FINISH:
- The character faces are warm gold (#EEC462) with a slightly deeper amber (#D8A038) along the lower part of each stroke — a simple flat two-tone split, not a smooth gradient.
- Every character has a THICK near-black outline (#1A1410) all the way around it, the same heavy linework the game's characters use.
- Behind the black outline there is one additional clean cream-white (#FCFCF6) border, offset evenly on all sides, so the title stays readable on a green background.
- Flat cel-shaded look. NO glossy highlights, NO shiny reflections, NO metallic sheen, NO smooth gradients, NO bevel, NO emboss, NO drop shadow, NO glow, NO sparkles, NO gemstones.

DECORATION: a small pair of simple crossed swords may sit centred directly ABOVE the row of characters, drawn in the same flat style with the same thick black outline, in cool steel grey with gold hilts. Keep it small and simple. No banners, no ribbons, no wings, no laurels, no frames around the text.

BACKGROUND: 100% solid flat MAGENTA (#FF00FF) everywhere around and between the letterforms. It is a chroma key and will be deleted. No shadow cast onto the background.

COMPOSITION: the lettering is centred and fills most of the image width, with a clear even magenta margin around it. Wide horizontal banner composition.
