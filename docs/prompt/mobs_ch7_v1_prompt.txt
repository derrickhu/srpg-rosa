A 2-column x 2-row grid of 4 CARTOON MONSTER CHARACTERS for a tactics game, drawn as game sprites.
The attached reference is DRAWING METHOD ONLY: thick near-black outline, flat cel fills, extreme
super-deformed mascot. Draw the four new creatures described below.

CRITICAL BACKGROUND: the entire background, including all space between and around the four
creatures, must be 100% solid flat MAGENTA, hex #FF00FF. It is a chroma key and will be deleted.
No white, no grey floor, no gradient, no ground shadow, no drop shadow.

CAMERA: a 3/4 top-down view. Every creature faces the viewer. Same eye level, same angle.

CRITICAL SIZE CONSTRAINT: displayed at only about 40 pixels tall. ONE bold chunky readable
silhouette each. Low-tier trash mobs, simpler than a hero. Head or main mass is about half
the height. The whole body fits a roughly square box.

CRITICAL CONTAINMENT: each creature INCLUDING wings, horns, and feet must stay inside the
central 60–68% of its own cell. Leave a wide magenta gutter. Wings and horns finish with
their tips well inside the cell.

STYLE: chunky rounded toy-like cartoon. THICK near-black outline (#1A1410) all the way around
every silhouette. FLAT fills, 4 to 6 colours. One light cream area (#F0F0E0) on each creature,
about 10–15% of the body. One highlight tone, one shadow tone. No gradients, no texture,
no belts, no rivets, no text.

PALETTE, anchored to real objects:
- Fog body: rainy-window glass, light cool gray-blue #C5D4DE, shadow #6E8AA0.
- Metal: an old Chinese coin, warm tarnished brass #C4923A, brighter coin edge #E8C878.
- Cream highlight: steamed-bun white #F0F0E0.
The palette is BRIGHT. Light fog plus warm coin-brass plus cream. Wide value range.

Per-cell contents:

TOP-LEFT — FOG LUMP (sword). A fat rounded teardrop of rainy-window gray-blue sitting on
the ground with NO legs. Cream belly patch. Two big round black eyes. The whole shape is
one smooth lump. Memory point: legless teardrop.

TOP-RIGHT — BRONZE OWL (bow). A round owl. Body is rainy-window gray-blue, chest is
steamed-bun cream. The beak is one big chunky old-coin brass triangle. Wings are SHORT
rounded fans, clearly finished teardrop tips, wider than the body, still inside the cell.
Two stubby feet. Memory point: wide short wings plus a brass beak.

BOTTOM-LEFT — MIST HOOF (cavalry). A low four-legged lizard-hound, body close to the
ground, longer than it is tall. Rainy-window gray-blue body, a cream stripe along the back.
Four short legs end in old-coin brass hooves. A short blunt snout. Memory point: low and wide.

BOTTOM-RIGHT — BELL SHELL (shield). A heavy squat DOME walker. The dome is old-coin brass,
with a steamed-bun cream patch on the front like a bell face. Four stubby feet fully visible
under the rim. A tiny round clapper dot on the cream face. Memory point: brass dome on stubby feet.

LAYOUT RULES:
1. EXACTLY 4 creatures in a clean 2x2.
2. NO borders, NO frames, NO platforms.
3. Each fills about 60–68% of its cell with magenta margin. Nothing crosses cells.
4. Same outline thickness, same simplification, same camera.
5. NO text, no labels, no watermark.
