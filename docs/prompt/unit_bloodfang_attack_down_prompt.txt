CRITICAL BACKGROUND REQUIREMENT, THIS OVERRIDES EVERYTHING ELSE: the background of the
image must be 100% solid flat MAGENTA, hex #FF00FF, bright pure pink-purple. NOT black. NOT
white. NOT grey. NOT a gradient. Every single pixel that is not part of the character must be
exactly magenta #FF00FF, including the areas between the cells. The background is a chroma
key and a black background makes the whole image unusable.

The attached reference image contains ONE SINGLE character frame. It defines only the
CHARACTER IDENTITY and the ART STYLE. It does NOT define the layout of the image you must
produce. Do NOT copy the reference's framing, and do NOT produce a walk cycle or a 4x4
sheet — produce the 2x2 attack sheet described below.

Match the reference exactly on: the same orc warlord, same horned iron helmet, same
ash-grey green skin, same tusks, same red-brown fur and leather, same round wooden shield,
same big notched iron cleaver with blood on the blade, same thick dark outlines, same flat
fills with light cel shading, same 3/4 top-down camera. Only the pose changes.

A 2x2 sprite sheet: 4 frames of a single heavy cleaver ATTACK, the character FACING DOWN
toward the camera the whole time. The swing is a COMPACT OVERHEAD CHOP straight down the
character's own centerline, NOT a wide horizontal sweep.

FRAME ORDER, reading left-to-right then top-to-bottom:
  Frame 1 (top-left):     WIND-UP. Cleaver held straight UP above the head, blade vertical and
                          pointing at the sky, elbows bent, knees bent, torso coiled.
  Frame 2 (top-right):    CHOP. Cleaver swung down to about chest height in front of the body,
                          blade still steeply angled, torso leaning forward.
  Frame 3 (bottom-left):  IMPACT. Cleaver driven DOWN in front of the feet, blade pointing at
                          the ground, arms extended downward, torso lunged forward, jaw open
                          in a roar. Strongest, most extreme pose.
  Frame 4 (bottom-right): RECOVERY. Cleaver lifted back to a neutral guard beside the body,
                          blade angled up, torso straightening.

ABSOLUTE RULES:
1. Background is 100% solid flat magenta #FF00FF everywhere, including between the cells.
   No gradient, no vignette, no ground shadow, no drop shadow, no black.
2. EXACTLY 4 equal-size cells in a 2x2 grid. Every cell the same width and height.
3. NO borders, NO dividing lines, NO gutters, NO frames between cells. Do not draw any line
   where the cells meet. Cells are separated only by empty magenta background.
4. IDENTICAL SCALE and IDENTICAL camera distance in all 4 cells. Same head size, same
   shoulder width. Do not zoom in on the action.
5. The character fills only about 45% of the cell height, centered in its cell, leaving a
   very wide empty magenta margin on all four sides. Draw it small. A small character with
   a lot of empty margin is CORRECT; a large character that fills the cell is WRONG.
6. NOTHING may touch or cross a cell edge or the outer edge of the image. Not a horn, not
   the cleaver tip, not the shield, not fur, not a boot. There must be clearly visible
   magenta between the cleaver tip and every edge in all 4 frames. Keep the cleaver inside
   the character's own vertical column — never let the blade reach out sideways past the
   shoulders. If a pose does not fit, make the character smaller.
7. Feet rest on the same horizontal baseline in every cell. The body may bob and lunge, but
   do not let the character float or leave the ground.
8. Draw the CHARACTER BODY ONLY. NO motion-blur streaks, NO slash arc, NO energy trail, NO
   impact flash, NO dust, NO sparks, NO blood spray, NO blood droplets in the air, NO hit
   effect of any kind. The swing VFX is drawn separately by the engine.
9. NO text of any kind anywhere in the image. Do NOT write the frame names, do NOT caption
   the cells, no labels, no numbers, no arrows, no UI, no watermark.
