A 4x4 sprite sheet, full 4-direction walk cycle, top-down tactics RPG.

The attached image is the EXACT character identity. Copy her: giant round head, stubby body,
honey-gold chunky hair, three ice-blue triangles on the bangs, ice-blue robe, cream collar,
jagged 4-triangle icicle hem, brown shoes, thick near-black outlines, flat cel fills.
Keep the ONE-HANDED open brown book hovering just above her raised mitten (viewer's left
when facing down). The book must slightly overlap the top of the palm so they stay ONE
connected silhouette. Left arm is a stub hanging down.

Do NOT redesign her. Do NOT make her prettier, slimmer, or more detailed.
Only facing and walk pose change.

ART STYLE (project style bible — do not deviate):
hand-drawn cartoon game sprite, thick near-black outlines (pure black or very dark warm
brown — never a mid-tone or colorful outline), flat saturated fills with light cel shading
(one highlight tone, one shadow tone), no gradients, no texture noise, no painterly
rendering, no photographic detail, 3/4 top-down camera matching a tactics grid.

PROPORTIONS — HEAD INCLUDING HAIR is about HALF total height. Tiny stubby body.
1.5-head mascot, square box. NOT a 2.5-head chibi. NOT a slim standing person.

WALK MOTION — the walk must be readable from the SILHOUETTE ALONE at 44 pixels tall.
Columns 1 and 3 must look obviously different from columns 2 and 4 even with all interior
detail removed. Understating this is the single most common failure:
  Column 1: neutral. Both feet together directly under the body, legs straight, body at its
            FULL standing height.
  Column 2: LEFT foot planted far FORWARD, right foot trailing far BEHIND, with a clearly
            VISIBLE GAP between the two feet — the stride is wide, not a shuffle. The whole
            body drops noticeably, squashing down about one tenth of its height. Arms swing
            opposite to the legs. Book stays glued to the raised-hand side.
  Column 3: neutral again, identical to column 1.
  Column 4: mirror of column 2 — RIGHT foot far forward, left foot trailing behind, same
            heavy body drop, arms swung the other way.
Exaggerate far past realism. A subtle, realistic walk reads as a statue sliding across the
board. If in doubt, make the stride wider and the body drop deeper.

ROWS:
  Row 1 top:    facing DOWN, face visible, book on viewer's left
  Row 2:        facing LEFT, left profile, book leading toward the left
  Row 3:        facing RIGHT, mirrored from row 2
  Row 4 bottom: facing UP, back of gold hair AND the open book still visible

RULES:
1. EXACTLY 16 equal cells, 4x4. No borders, no lines between cells.
2. Background 100% solid magenta #FF00FF. No shadow, no gradient.
3. Identical body scale and camera in every cell. Body never zooms.
4. Body fills about 50% of cell height, centered. Generous magenta margin.
5. Nothing crosses a cell edge. Same foot baseline.
6. Face: two dark oval eyes, two brows, tiny mouth. No nose, no blush.
7. 6 colors: ice blue, cream, gold hair, skin, book brown, outline.
8. NO purple/pink on costume. NO wizard hat. NO staff. NO fire orb.
9. NO TEXT, no labels, no watermark.
