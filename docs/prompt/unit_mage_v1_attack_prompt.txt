CRITICAL BACKGROUND REQUIREMENT, THIS OVERRIDES EVERYTHING ELSE: the background of the
image must be 100% solid flat MAGENTA, hex #FF00FF, bright pure pink-purple. NOT black. NOT
white. NOT grey. NOT a gradient. Every single pixel that is not part of the character must be
exactly magenta #FF00FF, including the areas between the cells. The background is a chroma
key and a black background makes the whole image unusable.

The attached reference image contains ONE SINGLE character frame. It defines only the
CHARACTER IDENTITY and the ART STYLE. It does NOT define the layout of the image you must
produce. Do NOT copy the reference's framing, and do NOT produce a walk cycle or a 4x4
sheet — produce the 2x2 attack sheet described below.

Match the reference exactly on: the same chibi girl mage, same deep-teal pointed hat, same
deep-teal robe, same cream collar, same warm brown hair, same tan boots, same thick brown
staff with a huge orange fire orb, same thick near-black outlines, same flat fills with
light cel shading, same 3/4 top-down camera, same giant-head-tiny-body proportions.
Only the pose changes.

A 2x2 sprite sheet: 4 frames of a single STAFF THRUST attack. <朝向段落>. She jabs the fire-orb
staff straight forward along her own centerline — NOT a wide horizontal sweep. The orb is the
thing that moves; the change between frames must be obvious in silhouette.

  朝下：the character FACING DOWN toward the camera the whole time. Face fully visible. She
        thrusts the staff FORWARD toward the viewer, orb leading, staying on her vertical
        centerline.
  朝左：the character seen in LEFT SIDE PROFILE, facing LEFT the whole time and thrusting toward
        the left. She stays in LEFT profile in all 4 frames — never turn her toward the camera,
        never flip her to face right.
  朝上：the character seen FROM BEHIND, facing UP and AWAY from the camera the whole time,
        thrusting away from the viewer. Her face is never visible. The giant pointed hat AND the
        orange orb must still be plainly visible in every frame.

FRAME ORDER, reading left-to-right then top-to-bottom:
  Frame 1 (top-left):     WIND-UP. Staff pulled back beside her shoulder, orb sitting near the
                          hat brim, knees bent, torso coiled.
  Frame 2 (top-right):    THRUST. Staff driven forward, orb at about chest height in front of
                          the body, torso leaning into the jab.
  Frame 3 (bottom-left):  IMPACT. Full extension — the orb is rammed as far forward as it goes,
                          arms stretched, leading foot planted, torso lunged. Strongest, most
                          extreme pose. Keep the staff on her own centerline; never let the orb
                          reach sideways past her shoulders.
  Frame 4 (bottom-right): RECOVERY. Staff lifted back to a neutral carry beside the body, orb
                          up, torso straightening.

ABSOLUTE RULES:
1. Background is 100% solid flat magenta #FF00FF everywhere, including between the cells.
   No gradient, no vignette, no ground shadow, no drop shadow, no black.
2. EXACTLY 4 equal-size cells in a 2x2 grid. Every cell the same width and height.
3. NO borders, NO dividing lines, NO gutters, NO frames between cells. Do not draw any line
   where the cells meet. Cells are separated only by empty magenta background.
4. IDENTICAL BODY SCALE and IDENTICAL camera distance in all 4 cells. Measure her from the
   top of her hair to the soles of her boots, ignoring the hat cone and the staff: that height
   must be the same in all 4 cells. Do not zoom in on the action.
5. Her BODY (hair-top to boots, hat cone and staff excluded) fills only about 40% of the cell
   height, centered in its cell, leaving a very wide empty magenta margin on all four sides.
   Draw it small. A small character with a lot of empty margin is CORRECT.
6. NOTHING may touch or cross a cell edge or the outer edge of the image — not the hat tip,
   not the orb, not a boot. There must be clearly visible magenta between the hat tip / orb
   and every edge in all 4 frames. Keep the staff inside the character's own vertical column.
   If a pose does not fit, make the character smaller.
7. Feet rest on the same horizontal baseline in every cell. The body may bob and lunge, but
   do not let the character float or leave the ground.
8. Draw the CHARACTER BODY ONLY. NO motion-blur streaks, NO energy trail, NO fire VFX, NO
   spell circle, NO impact flash, NO dust, NO sparks. The spell VFX is drawn separately by
   the engine.
9. NO purple, magenta, pink, or violet anywhere on the character (the background magenta is
   the key colour, not a costume colour).
10. NO text of any kind anywhere in the image. Do NOT write the frame names, do NOT caption
    the cells, no labels, no numbers, no arrows, no UI, no watermark.
