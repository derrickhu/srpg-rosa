CHARACTER: an orc warlord called the Bloodfang Chieftain. Chibi / super-deformed

CAMERA: 3/4 top-down view from slightly above, consistent in all 16 cells. You can see the
top of the head, the shoulders, and the full body down to the boots.

  Column 1: neutral standing pose, both feet together, weight low
  Column 2: LEFT foot stepping forward, heavy body bob down
  Column 3: neutral standing pose, both feet together

ABSOLUTE RULES:
1. EXACTLY 16 equal-size cells in a 4x4 grid. Every cell the same width and height.
2. NO borders, NO dividing lines, NO frames between cells. Cells are separated only by the
   flat background color.
3. Background is 100% solid flat magenta #FF00FF everywhere, including between the cells.
   No gradient, no vignette, no ground shadow, no drop shadow under the character.
4. IDENTICAL SCALE in all 16 cells: same head-to-boot height, same shoulder width, same
   camera distance. Do not zoom in or out between cells. Do not crop tighter in some cells.
5. IDENTICAL character identity, costume, and palette in all 16 cells.
6. Character is centered in its cell and fills about 70% of the cell height, leaving even
   magenta margin on all four sides.
7. Nothing crosses a cell edge — no tusk, cleaver, fur, bone, or boot may touch or overlap
   a neighboring cell.
8. Feet rest on the same horizontal baseline in every cell.
9. NO text, NO labels, NO numbers, NO arrows, NO UI, NO watermark anywhere in the image.
