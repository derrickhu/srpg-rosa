A 4x4 sprite sheet showing a full 4-direction walk cycle for a top-down tactics RPG unit.
Keep the character in the reference image: same face, same crimson tunic, same silver-white
breastplate, same brown hair, same palette. Only the facing direction, the walk pose and the
sword (described below) change.

HIS SWORD IS HIS WHOLE IDENTITY — in the reference image it is far too small. Fix that.
All of the following are mandatory:
- A HUGE TWO-HANDED GREATSWORD, LONGER THAN HE IS TALL. Carried resting on one shoulder at a
  clear diagonal, so his outline reads as a bold slash across the cell.
- The blade is BROAD and straight with a clean pointed tip, in bright polished silver-white,
  and there is a clear STRAIGHT CROSSGUARD at right angles to the blade above his grip.
  The broad blade plus the crossbar is what makes it read as a sword at tiny size — a thin
  blade with no crossguard turns into an unreadable stick.
- The blade tip rising well above his head is REQUIRED, not merely allowed. In game the
  character is scaled by his body height only, so a huge sword costs him nothing.
- Do not shrink the sword to fit the cell. Shrink the character instead.

PROPORTIONS — check against this, drawing him too tall is the most common failure:
- Extreme super-deformed mascot. His HEAD INCLUDING HAIR is about HALF of his body height.
  Body, arms and legs together are the other half: short, stubby, chunky, almost no neck.
- His BODY, from the top of his hair down to his boots and not counting the sword, fits in a
  roughly SQUARE box — about as wide as it is tall.
- This is NOT a 2-head or 2.5-head chibi. Think 1.5-head mascot: giant head, tiny body.

CHARACTER: a young knight.
- CRIMSON RED is his identity colour and covers most of his body.
- A plain SILVER-WHITE breastplate is his one light area, reading as near-white (#F0F0E0).
- Simple warm brown hair, warm tan boots.
- No cape, no belt, no pouch, no shield, no shoulder studs.

SIMPLIFY — characterise him with a few bold strokes and colours, never by piling on detail.
He displays about 48 pixels tall in game, where detail becomes noise:
- Only 4 to 6 flat colours on the whole character.
- NO belts, straps, buckles, pouches, rivets, armour seams, laces, stitching or trim.
- Face is minimal: two simple dark eyes, two eyebrows, at most a tiny mouth line. No nose,
  no ear detail, no blush.
- Arms and legs are simple rounded stubs; hands are mitten shapes with no fingers.

ART STYLE (project style bible — do not deviate):
hand-drawn cartoon game sprite, thick near-black outlines (pure black or very dark warm
brown — never a mid-tone or colorful outline), flat saturated fills with light cel shading
(one highlight tone, one shadow tone), no gradients, no texture noise, no painterly
rendering, no photographic detail.

BRIGHTNESS: bright saturated palette with a genuine near-white highlight. Never uniformly
dark or muted. Avoid grey, dusty, chalky or desaturated colours.

CAMERA: 3/4 top-down view from slightly above, consistent in all 16 cells. You can see the
top of the head, the shoulders, and the full body down to the boots.

SHEET LAYOUT — rows are facing direction, columns are walk frames:
  Row 1 (top):    facing DOWN, toward the camera, face fully visible
  Row 2:          facing LEFT, left side profile
  Row 3:          facing RIGHT, right side profile, mirrored from row 2
  Row 4 (bottom): facing UP, away from the camera, back of the head and armour visible.
                  The huge sword must still be plainly visible over his shoulder in this row.

ABSOLUTE RULES:
1. EXACTLY 16 equal-size cells in a 4x4 grid. Every cell the same width and height.
2. NO borders, NO dividing lines, NO frames between cells. Cells are separated only by the
   flat background color.
3. Background is 100% solid flat magenta #FF00FF everywhere, including between the cells.
   No gradient, no vignette, no ground shadow, no drop shadow under the character.
4. IDENTICAL BODY SCALE in all 16 cells — this is the single most important consistency
   rule. Measure him from the top of his hair to the soles of his boots, IGNORING the sword:
   that height must be the same in all 16 cells. The sword's apparent length changes with the
   pose and that is fine, but the body must never change size. Do not shrink the body in the
   cells where the sword happens to take up more room — let the sword run closer to the cell
   edge instead. Same camera distance in every cell; never zoom in or out between cells.
5. IDENTICAL character identity, costume, and palette in all 16 cells.
6. His BODY (hair-top to boots, sword excluded) fills about 45% of the cell height in EVERY
   cell. That 45% is fixed; the sword extends past it by whatever the pose needs.
   Centered with magenta margin on all four sides.
7. Nothing crosses a cell edge. The blade tip especially must stay clear of the edges.
8. Feet rest on the same horizontal baseline in every cell.
9. NO text, NO labels, NO numbers, NO arrows, NO UI, NO watermark anywhere in the image.
