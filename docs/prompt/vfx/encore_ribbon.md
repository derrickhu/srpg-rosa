# 再舞（encore_ribbon）—— 黑底 additive 绸环 9 帧（3x3）

- 技能：青弦 `encore`「再舞」，落在已经行动过的友军身上。
- 用途：`SKILL_VFX.encore.impact.set='encore_ribbon'`，`anchor='target' mode='burst'`。路径是玫瑰色的细光，不拿这张环去拉伸。
- 色相：红包红、素绸米白、金币黄。不要青绿（那是战场祝福）。
- 形态：一条绸带绕成松开的椭圆环，人可以站在环心里。不是四向光柱，不是十字，不是盾。

## Prompt

2D game VFX sprite sheet for a tactical SRPG. 3 columns by 3 rows, 9 equal cells, cells meet with no drawn borders.
Every pixel that is not the effect is pure black #000000.

One silk dance ribbon, seen from directly above, centered in each cell.
The ribbon is one thick graceful band, the red of a Chinese red envelope, with a cream highlight the color of undyed cotton along the inner edge, and a brilliant white core on the brightest part of the band.
A few tiny sparks the yellow of a gold coin drift beside the ribbon.
The shape is one flowing loop, a dancer's sash opening around an empty center, so a person could stand inside the loop.

Flat cel glow. Hard outer edge in saturated rose. Thin bright fringe. The band stays thick enough to read at a small size.

Frame order, left to right, then top to bottom:
  Frame 1: a short ribbon curl and one small white spark.
  Frame 2: the curl lengthens into a rising arc, about a third of a loop.
  Frame 3: PEAK. A loose oval loop of ribbon, brightest, three gold sparks.
  Frame 4: the loop a little wider, still bright, one extra soft fold.
  Frame 5: the loop opens, cream highlight still clear.
  Frame 6: the ribbon thins into two separate arcs.
  Frame 7: short rose arcs and two gold sparks drifting upward.
  Frame 8: a few small rose dashes.
  Frame 9: dim specks. Almost black.

The ribbon stays inside the central 60% of each cell, with black margin on every side.
The center of the loop stays on the cell center in every frame.
