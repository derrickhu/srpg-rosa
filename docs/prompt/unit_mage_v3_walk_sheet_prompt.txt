A 4x4 sprite sheet, full 4-direction walk cycle, top-down tactics RPG.
Keep the attached identity EXACTLY: same chunky 2-head girl mage, same deep-teal pointed hat
and robe, same cream collar, same warm brown bob, same fat orange-orb staff, same thick black
outlines, SAME two rounded tan boots. Only facing and walk pose change. Do NOT shrink her
into a toddler. Do NOT crop the robe into a flat hem with no boots.

FEET — mandatory in every cell:
- Two rounded tan-brown boots poke clearly below the teal robe, same as the identity frame.
- Leave a visible magenta gap under the soles. The robe hem must NEVER be the bottom of the
  drawing. If you are running out of room, shrink the whole character; do not cut the feet.

HER BODY (hair-top to boots, ignoring hat cone and staff) must stay the same size in all 16
cells and match the identity frame. The tall hat is EXTRA above the head.

ART STYLE (project style bible — do not deviate):
hand-drawn cartoon game sprite, chibi / super-deformed proportions with a large head
and short limbs, thick near-black outlines (pure black or very dark warm brown — never
a mid-tone or colorful outline), flat saturated fills with light cel shading (one
highlight tone, one shadow tone), no gradients, no texture noise, no painterly
rendering, no photographic detail, 3/4 top-down camera matching a tactics grid.
The sprite must stay readable at 40 pixels tall: silhouette first, exaggerate the
weapon and the one signature feature, drop everything that needs zoom to appreciate.
NO TEXT, no labels, no captions, no watermark anywhere in the image.

WALK MOTION — the walk must be readable from the SILHOUETTE ALONE at 44 pixels tall.
Columns 1 and 3 must look obviously different from columns 2 and 4 even with all interior
detail removed. Understating this is the single most common failure:
  Column 1: neutral. Both feet together directly under the body, legs straight, body at its
            FULL standing height.
  Column 2: LEFT foot planted far FORWARD, right foot trailing far BEHIND, with a clearly
            VISIBLE GAP between the two feet — the stride is wide, not a shuffle. The whole
            body drops noticeably, squashing down about one tenth of its height. Arms swing
            opposite to the legs.
  Column 3: neutral again, identical to column 1.
  Column 4: mirror of column 2 — RIGHT foot far forward, left foot trailing behind, same
            heavy body drop, arms swung the other way.
Exaggerate far past realism. A subtle, realistic walk reads as a statue sliding across the
board. If in doubt, make the stride wider and the body drop deeper.

ROWS:
  Row 1 top:    facing DOWN, face visible
  Row 2:        facing LEFT, left profile
  Row 3:        facing RIGHT, mirrored from row 2
  Row 4 bottom: facing UP, back of hat AND orange orb still visible

RULES:
1. EXACTLY 16 equal cells, 4x4. No borders, no lines between cells.
2. Background 100% solid magenta #FF00FF. No shadow, no gradient.
3. Identical body scale and camera in every cell. Hat/staff may change pose, body never zooms.
4. Body fills about 45% of cell height; hat extends above. Magenta margin on all sides,
   especially under the boots.
5. Nothing crosses a cell edge. Same foot baseline.
6. No purple/pink on costume. NO TEXT, no labels, no watermark.
