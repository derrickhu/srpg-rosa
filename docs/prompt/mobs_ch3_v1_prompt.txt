# 而建制感只有人形能给。代价是必须按严格口径和我方四职业逐个算配色。

# 配色推演（这一章是全五章唯一需要严格口径的）：
#   初稿定的「钢青 H200 L45」是**错的**：对我方盾卫 H210 L42 色相差 10、明度差 3，
#   两条判据都不过；而两者同为人形、同样举盾，是全游戏最容易混的一组。
#
#   改成：绿皮 #4DB36B H140 S40 L50  vs 弓手 H90 差 50°✓ / 法师 H180 差 40°✓ / 剑士 H24 差 116°✓
#         暗铁 #2A2E33 L15           vs 盾卫 H210 L42 明度差 27✓ / 城墙 L42 明度差 27✓
#         血红 #B23A32 H4 L45        饰色，点明血牙部族
#         骨白 #E8E4D8 L85           饰色
#
#   本章地形是城墙 H0 S0 L42（中性灰）+ 高地 H36 L66，所以怪必须有彩度才浮得出来，
#   绿皮正好；暗铁 L15 对灰墙 L42 靠明度立住。
#
# 同族小队没法靠色相互相区分——**统一色本身就是「建制」的视觉信号**。
#   所以四只的区分全压在剪影 + 一个饱和饰色上，这符合「剪影优先于细节」的第一法则。

# mob-1 血牙守卒(sword) / mob-2 城头弩手(bow) / mob-3 巡墙狼骑(cavalry) / mob-4 闸门盾卫(shield)

A 2-column x 2-row grid of 4 CARTOON ORC SOLDIER CHARACTERS for a tactics game, drawn as game
sprites. These are a UNIFORMED GARRISON — a real military unit holding a fortress, not wild beasts.

CRITICAL BACKGROUND: the entire background, including all space between and around the four
characters, must be 100% solid flat MAGENTA, hex #FF00FF. It is a chroma key and will be deleted.
No white, no grey, no gradient, no ground shadow, no drop shadow, no glow onto the background.
The characters must not touch each other or the edges of the image. This instruction overrides
everything else in this prompt.

CRITICAL FORBIDDEN COLOURS: absolutely NO pink, NO magenta, NO violet, NO purple, NO lilac anywhere
on any character. The background key colour is magenta, so any pinkish-purple will be destroyed when
the background is removed. Also NO steel blue and NO light blue anywhere — the player's own shield
hero is steel blue and these enemies must not be confused with him.

SHARED UNIFORM — all four must obviously belong to the same army:
- Skin is a mid GREEN (#4DB36B) with a darker green shadow tone. Every one of the four has this
  same green skin.
- Armour is DARK NEAR-BLACK IRON (#2A2E33) with a slightly lighter grey edge highlight. Same iron
  on all four.
- They are ORCS, not humans: hunched posture, heads pushed forward, arms too long and too thick for
  the body, two blunt tusks sticking up from the lower jaw, small angry eyes, no nose detail.

CAMERA: a 3/4 top-down view, as if looking down at a tabletop board from above and slightly in
front. Every character faces the viewer, standing on flat invisible ground. All four must sit at the
SAME eye level and be seen from the SAME angle.

CRITICAL SIZE CONSTRAINT: these are displayed at only about 40 pixels tall in the game. Each
character must be ONE bold chunky readable silhouette. NO fine detail, NO small parts, NO texture,
NO patterns, NO tiny highlights, NO belt buckles, NO straps, NO rivets, NO armour plate seams. If a
feature would be smaller than a tenth of the character's width, delete it.

CRITICAL PROPORTIONS: chibi / super-deformed. The head plus helmet is about HALF the total height,
the body and limbs the other half, almost no neck. The BODY (top of head to feet, not counting
weapons) must be nearly SQUARE — as wide as it is tall. Do NOT draw tall thin figures.

STYLE — must match the game's heroes:
- Chunky, rounded, toy-like cartoon characters. Slightly exaggerated, menacing but never gory.
- A THICK near-black outline (#1A1410) all the way around every character and around its major
  internal shapes.
- FLAT colour fills with light cel shading: one highlight tone, one shadow tone. No gradients, no
  soft shading, no gloss, no texture noise, no painterly brushwork, no pixel art.
- Weapons are EXAGGERATED oversized. Weapons may extend past the top of the head. Weapons must have
  real BULK — no thin sticks or wire-thin poles.
- Hands are mittens, no separate fingers.

Per-cell contents — each one gets ONE accent colour and ONE silhouette idea:

TOP-LEFT — CLEAVER TROOPER. A hunched orc infantryman gripping a huge heavy CLEAVER with a big
missing notch chopped out of the blade. The cleaver is broad and thick, held down and to the side,
and is nearly as tall as he is. Dark iron chest plate. His accent colour is a BLOOD RED (#B23A32)
cloth wrap tied around one shoulder. Compact, rounded, dense — the most "just a solid blocky
soldier" of the four.

TOP-RIGHT — CROSSBOW SHOOTER. An orc in a low CROUCH, holding a massive CROSSBOW sideways across his
body so that the crossbow's arms stick far out to the LEFT and RIGHT. The crossbow must be clearly
WIDER than his shoulders — the wide horizontal crossbow across a crouching body is the whole
silhouette idea, wide at the top and narrow at the bottom. The crossbow's stock and limbs are dark
iron; his accent colour is BONE WHITE (#E8E4D8) on a quiver of stubby bolts on his back.

BOTTOM-LEFT — WOLF RIDER. An orc riding on the back of a big four-legged CHARCOAL GREY wolf, seen
from the front three-quarter angle so all four wolf legs and the wolf's body length are visible. The
wolf stands low and long. The rider sits small and hunched on its back holding a short spear. The
wolf is dark charcoal grey fur, the rider has green skin and dark iron armour, and his accent colour
is a small BLOOD RED (#B23A32) pennant on the spear. The combined silhouette must be much WIDER than
it is tall.

BOTTOM-RIGHT — GATE SHIELD BEARER. An orc almost completely HIDDEN behind an enormous rectangular
IRON DOOR-SHIELD planted on the ground in front of him. The shield is a plain slab of dark near-black
iron with a thick lighter iron rim and three big chunky bolt studs, and it covers him from ankles to
chin — only the top of his tusked head and one hand gripping the shield edge are visible above and
beside it. The shield fills most of the cell and reads as a heavy flat SLAB. He has NO accent colour
— he is the darkest, plainest shape of the four. Do not make the shield rounded or cross-marked.

LAYOUT RULES:
1. EXACTLY 4 characters in a clean 2x2 arrangement.
2. NO borders, NO dividing lines, NO frames, NO circles or platforms under the characters.
3. Each character fills about 70% of its cell with a clear magenta margin on all four sides. Nothing
   crosses into a neighbouring cell.
4. All four must feel like ONE set drawn by one artist and like ONE army: same outline thickness,
   same green skin, same dark iron, same level of simplification, same camera angle.
5. NO text, no labels, no captions, no letters, no numbers, no watermark anywhere in the image.
