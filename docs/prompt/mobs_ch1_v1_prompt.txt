# 第一章杂兵 —— 四只魔物一张 2x2（generate2dsprite --mode grid --rows 2 --cols 2）
#
# 为什么四只挤一张：一次出全套，描边粗细、简化程度、俯视角度天然一致；
# 分四次生图必然漂移，而这四只是要并排站在同一块棋盘上的。
#
# 为什么只出一张静止姿势、不出行走/攻击/呼吸帧：
#   - 一章杂兵不值得每只做四方向。剩下的靠代码——没有 walk_* 时 AnimatedUnit.playWalk
#     退回 idle（棋子照样滑格移动），没有 attack_* 时退回代码突刺。
#   - 呼吸也不出图。两张几乎一样的 AI 帧之间必然抖动，画出来的呼吸读成画面在沸腾；
#     挤压拉伸是纯几何变换，交给 AnimatedUnit 做保证干净，还省掉每只三四帧图集。
#
# 配色约束（改之前先算一遍）：
#   - 避开草地色相 H70 附近，也避开我方四职业的专属色（深红/叶绿/钢蓝/金黄）。
#   - 菇伞必须是正红不能是洋红：品红抠色会把洋红一起吃掉。判据见
#     docs/美术管线-AI-sprite.md「素材本身带紫时，两个阈值都要抬」。
#   - 狼取近中性的炭灰，靠低饱和本身和钢蓝盾卫区分，不必再抢一个色相。
#
# 处理命令：
#   python3 ~/.cursor/skills/generate2dsprite/scripts/generate2dsprite.py process \
#     --input art/sprite-runs/mobs/raw-2x2.png --target creature --mode grid \
#     --rows 2 --cols 2 --label-prefix mob --cell-size 256 \
#     --output-dir art/sprite-runs/mobs/idle \
#     --align feet --shared-scale --component-mode largest --strict-qc \
#     --write-scale-profile art/sprite-runs/mobs/scale-profile.json
#   python3 scripts/despill-magenta.py art/sprite-runs/mobs/idle
#   node scripts/sprite2anim.mjs   # SETS 里 slime/sporecap/bloodwolf/rockshell 各取一帧
#
# mob-1 黏泥怪(sword) / mob-2 孢子菇(bow) / mob-3 血牙狼(cavalry) / mob-4 岩甲龟(shield)

A 2-column x 2-row grid of 4 CARTOON MONSTER CHARACTERS for a tactics game, drawn as game sprites.

CRITICAL BACKGROUND: the entire background, including all space between and around the four
creatures, must be 100% solid flat MAGENTA, hex #FF00FF. It is a chroma key and will be deleted.
No white, no grey, no gradient, no ground shadow, no drop shadow, no glow onto the background.
The creatures must not touch each other or the edges of the image.

CAMERA: a 3/4 top-down view, as if looking down at a tabletop board from above and slightly in
front. Every creature faces the viewer, standing on flat invisible ground. All four must sit at the
SAME eye level and be seen from the SAME angle.

CRITICAL SIZE CONSTRAINT: these are displayed at only about 40 pixels tall in the game. Each
creature must be ONE bold chunky readable silhouette. NO fine detail, NO small parts, NO texture,
NO patterns, NO tiny highlights. If a feature would be smaller than a tenth of the creature's
width, delete it. These are low-tier trash mobs and should look SIMPLER than a hero character —
fewer shapes, fewer colours, one strong idea each.

STYLE — must match the game's heroes:
- Chunky, rounded, toy-like cartoon creatures. Slightly exaggerated, friendly-menacing, never gory
  and never scary.
- A THICK near-black outline (#1A1410) all the way around every creature and around its major
  internal shapes.
- FLAT saturated colour fills. At most 3 colours per creature plus the outline, plus one lighter
  shade for a single simple cel-shaded highlight and one darker shade for a single shadow. No
  gradients, no soft shading, no gloss, no texture noise, no painterly brushwork, no pixel art.
- Big simple eyes. No noses, no ears, no fingers, no teeth smaller than the outline width.

Per-cell contents:

TOP-LEFT — SLIME. A fat rounded blob of jelly shaped like a wide teardrop or a squashed dome, wider
than it is tall, sitting directly on the ground with no legs. Body is a bright TURQUOISE BLUE
(#3ACCD8) with a lighter cyan top highlight. Two big simple round black eyes near the top. A small
blunt wobble or drip at the base of the blob. Nothing else — no mouth, no arms, no items.

TOP-RIGHT — MUSHROOM CREATURE. A big domed mushroom CAP that takes up the top two thirds of the
creature, bright RED (#D03828) with three or four large round CREAM WHITE spots on it. Under the
cap, a short fat cream-white stalk body with two stubby little feet and two big simple eyes peeking
out from under the cap's shadow. The cap must be clearly WIDER than the body — the wide cap is the
whole silhouette idea.

BOTTOM-LEFT — WOLF. A four-legged wolf standing low and long, seen from the front three-quarter
angle so all four legs and the body length are visible. Dark CHARCOAL GREY fur (#4A4450) with a
paler grey chest and muzzle. A big blocky head with two pointed ears, two glowing yellow eyes, and
two short white fangs showing at the mouth. A thick bushy tail sweeping out behind. Low, wide and
predatory — the silhouette should be much WIDER than it is tall.

BOTTOM-RIGHT — TORTOISE. A heavy armoured tortoise with an enormous domed SHELL covering almost the
whole creature. Shell is warm EARTH BROWN (#A87840) with a lighter tan rim and a few big chunky
plate divisions — no more than five plates, no fine mosaic. A small pale grey-green head pokes out
at the front with two little eyes, and four stubby pale legs at the corners. The shell must
dominate — it should read as a walking dome.

LAYOUT RULES:
1. EXACTLY 4 creatures in a clean 2x2 arrangement.
2. NO borders, NO dividing lines, NO frames, NO circles or platforms under the creatures.
3. Each creature fills about 70% of its cell with a clear magenta margin on all four sides. Nothing
   crosses into a neighbouring cell.
4. All four must feel like ONE set drawn by one artist: same outline thickness, same level of
   simplification, same camera angle.
5. NO text, no labels, no captions, no letters, no numbers, no watermark anywhere in the image.
