# 风格试点：验证「我方人类提亮 + 强化记忆点」方向。风格段落取自 _style_block_v2.txt。
# 相对 v1 的三处改动：
#   1. 加奶白斗篷做亮区，解决 v1 中位明度只有 26、在草地上迷彩化的问题
#   2. 绿色降为兜帽/护腕的职业标识色，不再是主导明度
#   3. 记忆点：背后夸张的大羽箭扇（40px 下是可辨识的扇形剪影）
# 键色：#FF00FF —— 角色主色为奶白/绿/棕，与品红色相距远
# 下游：generate2dsprite.py process --target player --mode player_sheet --align feet
#       --shared-scale --component-mode largest

A 4x4 sprite sheet showing a full 4-direction walk cycle for a top-down tactics RPG unit.

CHARACTER: a cheerful young forest archer. Chibi / super-deformed proportions with a large
head and short body, roughly 2.5 heads tall.
- SIGNATURE FEATURE (must be unmistakable at tiny size): an OVERSIZED recurve bow, about as
  tall as the character from head to boot, in warm honey-wood with cream-wrapped grip and
  pale bowstring. Held VERTICALLY alongside the body, close in, so it lengthens the
  silhouette without widening it. The bow is the character's identity — make it big, make
  its double-curve shape clearly readable, and never let it be hidden behind the body.
- BRIGHT AREA (required): a cream / off-white short cape with a rounded collar over the
  shoulders, roughly 12% of the body. This is the lightest thing on the character.
- Leaf-green hood pushed back off the head, green bracers, warm tan boots and belt.
- Warm reddish-brown hair, simple bright confident face with thick dark eyes.
- A COMPACT quiver on the back with just three short feather tips peeking above the
  shoulder. These feathers must be SMALLER THAN THE HEAD and must not spread sideways —
  they are a small accent, not a fan, not wings, and never a headdress around the head.

ART STYLE (project style bible — do not deviate):
hand-drawn cartoon game sprite, chibi / super-deformed proportions with a large head
and short limbs, thick near-black outlines (pure black or very dark warm brown — never
a mid-tone or colorful outline), flat saturated fills with light cel shading (one
highlight tone, one shadow tone), no gradients, no texture noise, no painterly
rendering, no photographic detail, 3/4 top-down camera matching a tactics grid.

BRIGHTNESS AND VALUE RANGE — this is what separates a cheerful palette from a muddy
one, and it is the most common failure:
- The palette must be BRIGHT and span a wide value range. Saturated mid-tones plus a
  genuine near-white highlight.
- The cream cape must read as near-white (#F0F0E0 / #E0E0D0), not beige and not grey.
- Never render the character in uniformly dark or muted tones.
- Avoid grey, avoid dusty, chalky or desaturated colors. Avoid olive-drab and khaki.
- Warm gold accent: #F0C060.

CAMERA: 3/4 top-down view from slightly above, consistent in all 16 cells. You can see the
top of the head, the shoulders, and the full body down to the boots.

SHEET LAYOUT — rows are facing direction, columns are walk frames:
  Row 1 (top):    facing DOWN, toward the camera, face fully visible
  Row 2:          facing LEFT, left side profile
  Row 3:          facing RIGHT, right side profile, mirrored from row 2
  Row 4 (bottom): facing UP, away from the camera, back of the cape and the feather fan visible
  Column 1: neutral standing pose, both feet together
  Column 2: LEFT foot stepping forward, slight body bob down
  Column 3: neutral standing pose, both feet together
  Column 4: RIGHT foot stepping forward, slight body bob down

ABSOLUTE RULES:
1. EXACTLY 16 equal-size cells in a 4x4 grid. Every cell the same width and height.
2. NO borders, NO dividing lines, NO frames between cells. Cells are separated only by the
   flat background color.
3. Background is 100% solid flat magenta #FF00FF everywhere, including between the cells.
   No gradient, no vignette, no ground shadow, no drop shadow under the character.
4. IDENTICAL SCALE in all 16 cells: same head-to-boot height, same shoulder width, same
   camera distance. Do not zoom in or out between cells. Do not crop tighter in some cells.
5. IDENTICAL character identity, costume, and palette in all 16 cells.
6. Character INCLUDING THE BOW is centered in its cell and fills about 55% of the cell
   height, leaving generous even magenta margin on all four sides.
7. Nothing crosses a cell edge — no hair, cape, bow, quiver, feather, boot, or hand may touch
   or overlap a neighboring cell. The bow tips especially must stay well clear of the top and
   bottom edges. If in doubt, draw the character smaller.
8. Feet rest on the same horizontal baseline in every cell.
9. NO text, NO labels, NO numbers, NO arrows, NO UI, NO watermark anywhere in the image.
