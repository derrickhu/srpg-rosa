# mob-1 藤缚茧(sword) / mob-2 喷孢囊(bow) / mob-3 林影豹(cavalry) / mob-4 苔石像(shield)

A 2-column x 2-row grid of 4 CARTOON PLANT-CREATURE CHARACTERS for a tactics game, drawn as game
sprites. These are things the forest itself grew, not animals wearing armour.

CRITICAL BACKGROUND: the entire background, including all space between and around the four
creatures, must be 100% solid flat MAGENTA, hex #FF00FF. It is a chroma key and will be deleted.
No white, no grey, no gradient, no ground shadow, no drop shadow, no glow onto the background.
The creatures must not touch each other or the edges of the image. This instruction overrides
everything else in this prompt.

CRITICAL FORBIDDEN COLOURS: absolutely NO pink, NO magenta, NO violet, NO purple, NO lilac anywhere
on any creature. The background key colour is magenta, so any pinkish-purple on a creature will be
destroyed when the background is removed. Use only the specific colours named per cell below.

CAMERA: a 3/4 top-down view, as if looking down at a tabletop board from above and slightly in
front. Every creature faces the viewer, standing on flat invisible ground. All four must sit at the
SAME eye level and be seen from the SAME angle.

CRITICAL SIZE CONSTRAINT: these are displayed at only about 40 pixels tall in the game. Each
creature must be ONE bold chunky readable silhouette. NO fine detail, NO small parts, NO texture,
NO patterns, NO tiny highlights. If a feature would be smaller than a tenth of the creature's
width, delete it. These are low-tier trash mobs and should look SIMPLER than a hero character —
fewer shapes, fewer colours, one strong idea each.

STYLE — must match the game's heroes:
- Chunky, rounded, toy-like cartoon creatures. Slightly exaggerated, friendly-menacing, never gory
  and never scary.
- A THICK near-black outline (#1A1410) all the way around every creature and around its major
  internal shapes.
- FLAT saturated colour fills. At most 3 colours per creature plus the outline, plus one lighter
  shade for a single simple cel-shaded highlight and one darker shade for a single shadow. No
  gradients, no soft shading, no gloss, no texture noise, no painterly brushwork, no pixel art.
- Any "glowing" part must be painted as a FLAT BRIGHT SHAPE with a hard outline. No soft glow, no
  halo, no bloom, no light spill.
- Big simple eyes where the creature has eyes at all. No noses, no ears, no fingers, no teeth
  smaller than the outline width.

Per-cell contents:

TOP-LEFT — VINE COCOON. A fat rounded ball of thick woody vines wound tightly around something,
wider than it is tall, sitting directly on the ground with no legs and NO FACE AT ALL — no eyes, no
mouth. The vines are a pale sage grey-green (#98CDB3) with a slightly darker sage shadow underneath.
Between the vines are three or four narrow FLAT BRIGHT CYAN slits, painted as hard-edged solid
shapes with dark outlines, as if something lit is sealed inside. A few short vine ends curl out at
the base. The "no face, just a wound-up ball with glowing cracks" idea is the whole silhouette.

TOP-RIGHT — DROOPING SPORE SAC. A plant whose thin stalk rises from the ground and then BENDS OVER
so that a big heavy bulbous sac HANGS DOWNWARD at the front. The sac is the bottom-heavy part of the
silhouette: narrow at the top where the stalk is, fat and round at the bottom. The sac is a warm
BONE WHITE (#E8E4D8) with a darker cream shadow. At the very bottom of the sac is a single dark
split opening, like a puckered slit mouth, painted in near-black. Two small simple eyes on the sac
above the split. CRITICAL: this must read as TOP-NARROW and BOTTOM-WIDE — it is the opposite of a
normal upright mushroom cap.

BOTTOM-LEFT — PANTHER. A four-legged big cat standing low and long, seen from the front
three-quarter angle so all four legs and the body length are visible. Fur is a warm RUST ORANGE-RED
(#C65A39) with a darker rust shadow along the belly. Running down its spine from head to tail is a
single row of four or five flat BONE WHITE LEAF-SHAPED FINS, like pale leaves standing up out of its
back — this row of pale fins is the memorable feature and must be clearly visible against the rust
fur. A blocky head with two small ears and two simple eyes. Low, wide and predatory — the silhouette
should be much WIDER than it is tall.

BOTTOM-RIGHT — MOSS STATUE. A squat crouching stone idol with a big BLOCKY SQUARE HEAD that sits
directly on its shoulders with no neck, and short thick stubby arms and legs folded in close, so the
whole thing reads as a heavy rounded-off cube. The stone is a dark grey-green (#4D8066) with a
lighter grey-green highlight on the top of the head. On top of its shoulders and head sits a thick
slab of brighter moss, painted as one simple flat shape, not many little tufts. Two small simple
eyes carved into the square face. The heavy cube silhouette must dominate.

LAYOUT RULES:
1. EXACTLY 4 creatures in a clean 2x2 arrangement.
2. NO borders, NO dividing lines, NO frames, NO circles or platforms under the creatures.
3. Each creature fills about 70% of its cell with a clear magenta margin on all four sides. Nothing
   crosses into a neighbouring cell.
4. All four must feel like ONE set drawn by one artist: same outline thickness, same level of
   simplification, same camera angle.
5. NO text, no labels, no captions, no letters, no numbers, no watermark anywhere in the image.
