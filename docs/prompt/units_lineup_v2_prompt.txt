A character lineup sheet for a top-down tactics RPG: FOUR different hero units standing
side by side in a single row, all facing the camera (front view), all the same size and
standing on the same baseline.

PROPORTIONS — check against this, drawing them too tall is the most common failure:
- Extreme super-deformed mascot. The HEAD INCLUDING HAIR OR HELMET is about HALF of each
  character's total height. Body, arms and legs together are the other half: short,
  stubby, chunky.
- Each character fits in a roughly SQUARE box — total width about 0.9 to 1.0 times total
  height. If any of them looks like a slim standing person, it is wrong.
- This is NOT a 2-head or 2.5-head chibi. Think 1.5-head mascot: giant head, tiny body.

SIMPLIFY — characterise with a few bold strokes and colours, never by piling on detail.
These are displayed about 48 pixels tall, where detail becomes noise and makes the
character read smaller and busier:
- Only 4 to 6 flat colours on each character. ONE of them dominates the body and is that
  character's identity colour.
- NO belts, straps, buckles, pouches, rivets, armour seams, laces, stitching, patterns,
  engraving or trim. Delete every small accessory.
- Face is minimal: two simple dark eyes, two eyebrows, at most a tiny mouth line. No nose,
  no ear detail, no blush.
- Arms and legs are simple rounded stubs; hands are mitten shapes, no fingers.
- Each signature prop is ONE big clean simple silhouette with no ornament on it.
- Each character must read as a handful of bold flat shapes, not as an illustration.

FROM LEFT TO RIGHT — each described by its colour and its ONE prop, nothing more:

1. SWORDSMAN — CRIMSON RED is his identity colour and covers most of his body. A plain
   silver-white chest plate is his one light area. His prop: an OVERSIZED plain greatsword,
   longer than he is tall, resting on one shoulder at a diagonal so the outline reads as a
   bold slash. Simple brown hair. Nothing else.

2. ARCHER — LEAF GREEN is his identity colour and covers most of his body, including a
   simple pointed hood. A plain cream / off-white short cape is his one light area. His
   prop: an OVERSIZED plain honey-wood recurve bow, about as tall as he is, held vertically
   alongside the body. Nothing else — no quiver, no arrows, no feathers.

3. SHIELD BEARER — STEEL BLUE is his identity colour and covers most of his body. His prop:
   one huge plain rectangular TOWER SHIELD, wider than his body and nearly as tall, planted
   in front of him so his outline reads as a solid rectangle. The shield face is plain
   off-white with a single bold simple emblem shape — this is his light area. Nothing else.

4. LANCER ON HORSEBACK — WARM GOLDEN ORANGE is his identity colour and covers most of the
   rider. He sits on a plain WHITE horse, which is his light area. His prop: one long plain
   lance held upright. Being mounted makes his outline the widest and lowest of the four —
   clearly a different shape from the three foot soldiers. Nothing else.

ART STYLE (project style bible — do not deviate):
hand-drawn cartoon game sprite, thick near-black outlines (pure black or very dark warm
brown — never a mid-tone or colorful outline), flat saturated fills with light cel shading
(one highlight tone, one shadow tone), no gradients, no texture noise, no painterly
rendering, no photographic detail, 3/4 top-down camera matching a tactics grid.

BRIGHTNESS: the palette must be BRIGHT and saturated with a genuine near-white highlight
on every character. Never uniformly dark or muted. Avoid grey, dusty, chalky or
desaturated colours. Avoid olive-drab and khaki. Cream anchors #F0F0E0 / #E0E0D0.

COLOR SEPARATION (hard requirement): the four heroes must be immediately distinguishable
by colour alone. Crimson red / leaf green / steel blue / warm gold. Do not let any two of
them drift toward the same hue, and do not use olive or khaki on any of them.

ABSOLUTE RULES:
1. Exactly FOUR characters in ONE horizontal row, evenly spaced, none overlapping.
2. Background is 100% solid flat magenta #FF00FF everywhere, including between characters.
   No gradient, no vignette, no ground shadow, no drop shadow under the characters.
3. All four the same scale and the same camera distance. Feet (and hooves) on one shared
   horizontal baseline.
4. Each character including his prop must sit fully inside its own quarter of the image
   with clear magenta margin all around. Nothing touches the image edge.
5. NO text, NO labels, NO names, NO numbers, NO UI, NO watermark anywhere in the image.
