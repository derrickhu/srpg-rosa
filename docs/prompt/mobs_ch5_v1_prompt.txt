A 2-column x 2-row grid of 4 CARTOON VOLCANIC CREATURE CHARACTERS for a tactics game, drawn as game
sprites. These live on a volcanic dragon ridge.

CRITICAL BACKGROUND: the entire background, including all space between and around the four
creatures, must be 100% solid flat MAGENTA, hex #FF00FF. It is a chroma key and will be deleted.
No white, no grey, no gradient, no ground shadow, no drop shadow, no glow onto the background.
The creatures must not touch each other or the edges of the image. This instruction overrides
everything else in this prompt.

CRITICAL FORBIDDEN COLOURS: absolutely NO pink, NO magenta, NO violet, NO purple, NO lilac anywhere
on any creature. The background key colour is magenta, so any pinkish-purple will be destroyed when
the background is removed. Lava must be ORANGE-RED, never pinkish-red and never purple-red.

CRITICAL — HOW TO DRAW GLOWING LAVA: the glowing cracks must be painted as FLAT BRIGHT SOLID SHAPES
with HARD EDGES and a dark outline around them, like cut-out pieces of bright orange paper laid into
the rock. Absolutely NO soft glow, NO halo, NO bloom, NO light spilling onto nearby surfaces, NO
gradient fade, NO blur. This is a hard style rule.

CAMERA: a 3/4 top-down view, as if looking down at a tabletop board from above and slightly in
front. Every creature faces the viewer, standing on flat invisible ground. All four must sit at the
SAME eye level and be seen from the SAME angle.

CRITICAL SIZE CONSTRAINT: these are displayed at only about 40 pixels tall in the game. Each
creature must be ONE bold chunky readable silhouette. NO fine detail, NO small parts, NO texture,
NO patterns, NO tiny highlights. If a feature would be smaller than a tenth of the creature's
width, delete it. These are low-tier trash mobs and should look SIMPLER than a hero character —
fewer shapes, fewer colours, one strong idea each.

STYLE — must match the game's heroes:
- Chunky, rounded, toy-like cartoon creatures. Slightly exaggerated, friendly-menacing, never gory
  and never scary.
- A THICK near-black outline (#1A1410) all the way around every creature and around its major
  internal shapes.
- FLAT colour fills. At most 3 colours per creature plus the outline, plus one lighter shade for a
  single simple cel-shaded highlight and one darker shade for a single shadow. No gradients, no soft
  shading, NO GLOSSY SHINE, no texture noise, no painterly brushwork, no pixel art.
- Big simple eyes where the creature has eyes at all. No noses, no ears, no separate fingers.

Per-cell contents:

TOP-LEFT — LAVA BOULDER. A squat rounded lump of black volcanic rock sitting DIRECTLY on the ground
with NO legs and NO arms, wider than it is tall, like a big rounded stone. The rock is almost black
obsidian (#1F1B1A). Across its surface run four or five wide BRIGHT ORANGE-RED (#FF6A1F) CRACKS,
painted as flat hard-edged solid shapes with dark outlines. The cracks should be thick and few, not
a fine web. Two small simple glowing orange eyes near the top, drawn as flat shapes. This is the
darkest body with the brightest markings — the highest contrast of the four.

TOP-RIGHT — EMBER BAT. A small bat whose ENORMOUS SPREAD WINGS fill the top two thirds of the
silhouette while its body is tiny by comparison, so the shape is very WIDE AT THE TOP and narrow at
the bottom. The wings are broad, rounded and leathery with three thick finger-struts each — no thin
membranes, no scalloped fringe detail. Wings and body are a warm ORANGE-RED (#E2622A) with a darker
rust shadow on the underside of the wings. A blunt little head with two big simple eyes and two
short ears. The wings must be clearly wider than the body is tall.

BOTTOM-LEFT — ROCK-SCALE DRAKE. A four-legged lizard-beast standing low and long, seen from the
front three-quarter angle so all four legs and the body length are visible. Its hide is a
DESATURATED DARK SLATE GREY (#4A4A50) — almost colourless, the only grey creature here — with a few
big chunky flat rock plates on the flanks. Running along its spine from neck to tail is a single row
of five thick BRIGHT ORANGE-RED (#FF6A1F) spikes, painted as flat hard-edged shapes. A blocky head
with a heavy jaw and two simple eyes. The silhouette should be much WIDER than it is tall.

BOTTOM-RIGHT — ASH BEETLE. A heavy beetle whose enormous smooth domed SHELL covers almost the whole
creature, reading as a walking dome. The shell is a PALE COOL ASH GREY (#B0B8C0) with a slightly
lighter rim and a single simple seam down the middle — no more than three big plate divisions. Four
stubby pale legs at the corners and two small simple dark eyes peeking out at the front under the
shell. CRITICAL: this creature has NO glowing lava, NO orange, NO cracks at all — it is the one pale
non-glowing shape in the set, and that is exactly what makes it readable. The dome must dominate.

LAYOUT RULES:

2. NO borders, NO dividing lines, NO frames, NO circles or platforms under the creatures.
3. Each creature fills about 70% of its cell with a clear magenta margin on all four sides. Nothing

4. All four must feel like ONE set drawn by one artist: same outline thickness, same level of

5. NO text, no labels, no captions, no letters, no numbers, no watermark anywhere in the image.
