A full-screen PORTRAIT (2:3) background for a roadside supply-shop scene in a cartoon fantasy tactics mobile game.

CAMERA — eye-level / slight front view looking into a sunny grassland clearing.
NOT top-down. NOT bird's-eye. Trees and bushes must be seen from the SIDE
(trunk + rounded leafy crown), never as flat circular canopies from above.

COMPOSITION:
- LOWER 45%: open warm yellow-green grass and a soft pale dirt patch in the center —
  this is empty stage space where a merchant and stall will be placed in code.
  Keep it clean, low detail, no props.
- MIDDLE: gently rising grass, a few chunky round side-view bushes left and right.
- UPPER: soft rolling hills on the horizon, calm pale sky with 2–3 simple flat clouds.
  Horizon allowed (this is a scene poster, not a tactics board).

COLOUR — match chapter grassland:
- Grass BRIGHT warm sunny yellow-green #B4D45A, #C2DE6E, #A8CC4E
- Bushes deeper green with one cel highlight
- Dirt path pale cream-tan #E8D8A8
- Sky soft light blue, flat fills

STYLE:
- Hand-drawn cartoon, thick near-black outlines on bushes/hills only (grass can be softer)
- Flat saturated fills, light cel shading, NO gradients, NO texture noise, NO painterly,
  NO photographic detail
- Friendly toy-like shapes

NO characters, NO merchant, NO stall, NO goods, NO price tags, NO text, NO watermark,
NO UI frames, NO phone bezel.
