#
# 取代 mage/idle 单帧捷径。风格、比例、键色一律对齐剑士/弓手 v3，不要再单独出一张精致立绘。
#
# 定色（美术风格圣经 §2.1）：
#   主色 DEEP TEAL `#0E7A7A` H180 S79 L27。
#   对剑士 H24 / 弓手 H90 / 骑兵 H42 色相差都 >80°；对盾卫 H210 差 30°（≥25°）。
#   禁止紫/粉/品红——despill-magenta 会把它们当溢色压掉。橙火球是道具色，不是主色。
#
# 剪影一句话：一顶高过全身的三角尖帽扣在近正方形的短肢身体上，身旁一根头一样大的火球杖。
# 唯一记忆点：那顶失真的大尖帽（形状，不是颜色）。火球杖是武器可读性，帽子才是 40px 下的身份。

# 键色：#FF00FF —— 主色青绿/棕/橙，与品红欧氏距离远。
# 下游：generate2dsprite.py process --target player --mode player_sheet --align feet
#       --shared-scale --component-mode largest --write-scale-profile
# 参考图：art/sprite-runs/mage/identity-ref-down.png（单帧，只锁身份）

A 4x4 sprite sheet showing a full 4-direction walk cycle for a top-down tactics RPG unit.
Keep the character in the reference image: same face, same deep-teal pointed hat, same deep-teal
robe, same cream collar, same warm brown hair, same huge orange fire-orb staff, same palette.
Only the facing direction, the walk pose and how the staff sits change.

HER POINTED HAT IS HER WHOLE IDENTITY — it must be huge.
All of the following are mandatory:
- A GIANT WITCH HAT, a tall sharp triangle. From brim to tip the hat is about as tall as the rest
  of her body. The brim is a wide disc sitting on the head. At tiny size this triangle-on-a-square
  is the only way to read "mage" — a small cute hat turns her into a generic child.
- The hat tip rising well above her head is REQUIRED, not merely allowed. In game the character is
  scaled by her body height only (hat excluded from bodySpan), so a huge hat costs her nothing.
- Do not shrink the hat to fit the cell. Shrink the character instead.

HER STAFF is the weapon, not the identity, but it must still read at 40px:
- A thick brown wooden stick held close to the body, with a FAT ROUND ORANGE FIRE ORB on top
  (bright #F08C2A with a cream highlight). The orb is about as big as her head. A thin stick with
  a tiny gem becomes an unreadable line.
- The orb may rise above the hat brim; that is fine. Keep the staff inside its own cell.

PROPORTIONS — check against this, drawing her too tall is the most common failure:
- Extreme super-deformed mascot. Her HEAD INCLUDING HAIR (not counting the hat cone) is about
  HALF of her body height. Body, arms and legs together are the other half: short, stubby,
  chunky, almost no neck.
- Her BODY, from the top of her hair down to her boots and not counting the hat cone or staff,
  fits in a roughly SQUARE box — about as wide as it is tall.
- This is NOT a 2-head or 2.5-head chibi. Think 1.5-head mascot: giant head, tiny body.
- She is a toy-soldier child, NOT a pretty teen, NOT a slim anime girl.

CHARACTER: a young girl mage.
- DEEP TEAL `#0E7A7A` is her identity colour and covers most of her body: the robe AND the hat.
- A plain CREAM collar is her one light area, reading as near-white (#F0F0E0).
- Simple warm brown hair in a short bob, two chunky bangs, warm tan boots.
- No cape, no belt, no pouch, no stars, no moons, no runes, no sparkles on the clothes.

SIMPLIFY — characterise her with a few bold strokes and colours, never by piling on detail.
She displays about 48 pixels tall in game, where detail becomes noise:
- Only 4 to 6 flat colours on the whole character: deep teal, cream, skin #EEC49A, warm brown,
  orange orb, near-black outline.
- NO belts, straps, buckles, pouches, rivets, seams, laces, stitching, patterns, stars or trim.
- Face is minimal: two simple dark eyes, two eyebrows, at most a tiny mouth line. No nose,
  no ear detail, no blush, no eyelashes, no pupils with highlight.
- Arms and legs are simple rounded stubs; hands are mitten shapes with no fingers.
- NO purple, magenta, pink, or violet anywhere on the character.

ART STYLE (project style bible — do not deviate):
hand-drawn cartoon game sprite, chibi / super-deformed proportions with a large head
and short limbs, thick near-black outlines (pure black or very dark warm brown — never
a mid-tone or colorful outline), flat saturated fills with light cel shading (one
highlight tone, one shadow tone), no gradients, no texture noise, no painterly
rendering, no photographic detail, 3/4 top-down camera matching a tactics grid.
The sprite must stay readable at 40 pixels tall: silhouette first, exaggerate the
weapon and the one signature feature, drop everything that needs zoom to appreciate.
NO TEXT, no labels, no captions, no watermark anywhere in the image.

BRIGHTNESS: bright saturated palette with a genuine near-white highlight on the cream collar
and the fire orb. Never uniformly dark or muted. Avoid grey, dusty, chalky or desaturated colours.

CAMERA: 3/4 top-down view from slightly above, consistent in all 16 cells. You can see the
top of the hat, the shoulders, and the full body down to the boots.

SHEET LAYOUT — rows are facing direction, columns are walk frames:
  Row 1 (top):    facing DOWN, toward the camera, face fully visible
  Row 2:          facing LEFT, left side profile
  Row 3:          facing RIGHT, right side profile, mirrored from row 2
  Row 4 (bottom): facing UP, away from the camera, back of the hat and robe visible.
                  The giant pointed hat AND the orange orb must still be plainly visible in this
                  row — a back view that is just a teal triangle has no weapon identity.
WALK MOTION — the walk must be readable from the SILHOUETTE ALONE at 44 pixels tall.
Columns 1 and 3 must look obviously different from columns 2 and 4 even with all interior
detail removed. Understating this is the single most common failure:
  Column 1: neutral. Both feet together directly under the body, legs straight, body at its
            FULL standing height.
  Column 2: LEFT foot planted far FORWARD, right foot trailing far BEHIND, with a clearly
            VISIBLE GAP between the two feet — the stride is wide, not a shuffle. The whole
            body drops noticeably, squashing down about one tenth of its height. Arms swing
            opposite to the legs.
  Column 3: neutral again, identical to column 1.
  Column 4: mirror of column 2 — RIGHT foot far forward, left foot trailing behind, same
            heavy body drop, arms swung the other way.
Exaggerate far past realism. A subtle, realistic walk reads as a statue sliding across the
board. If in doubt, make the stride wider and the body drop deeper.

ABSOLUTE RULES:
1. EXACTLY 16 equal-size cells in a 4x4 grid. Every cell the same width and height.
2. NO borders, NO dividing lines, NO frames between cells. Cells are separated only by the
   flat background color.
3. Background is 100% solid flat magenta #FF00FF everywhere, including between the cells.
   No gradient, no vignette, no ground shadow, no drop shadow under the character.
4. IDENTICAL BODY SCALE in all 16 cells — this is the single most important consistency
   rule. Measure her from the top of her hair to the soles of her boots, IGNORING the hat cone
   and the staff: that height must be the same in all 16 cells. The hat and orb may change
   apparent length with the pose, but the body must never change size. Do not shrink the body
   in the cells where the hat happens to take up more room — let the hat run closer to the cell
   edge instead. Same camera distance in every cell; never zoom in or out between cells.
5. IDENTICAL character identity, costume, and palette in all 16 cells.
6. Her BODY (hair-top to boots, hat cone and staff excluded) fills about 45% of the cell height
   in EVERY cell. That 45% is fixed; the hat extends past it by whatever the pose needs.
   Centered with magenta margin on all four sides.
7. Nothing crosses a cell edge. The hat tip and the orb especially must stay clear of the edges.
8. Feet rest on the same horizontal baseline in every cell.
9. NO text, NO labels, NO numbers, NO arrows, NO UI, NO watermark anywhere in the image.
