# 剑士 v3 —— 朝下（面向镜头）挥砍 4 帧（2x2，generate2dsprite --mode attack）
#
# 参考图：art/sprite-runs/sword/identity-ref-down.png —— 单帧，只锁身份+风格。
#   不要传 4x4 的行走 sheet 当参考图：布局锚定会压过 2x2 指令，模型会直接复刻行走 sheet
#   （血牙酋长踩过这个坑）。
# 处理时用 --scale-profile art/sprite-runs/sword/scale-profile.json 复用行走 sheet 的缩放，
# 否则播放时会在行走与攻击之间突然变大变小。
#
# 巨剑很长，挥砍时横扫会一路扫出格子。所以这里指定**沿自身中线的竖直劈砍**，
# 和血牙的处理一样——不是为了迁就格子大小（那个约束已经废了），而是 2x2 切帧要求
# 每帧留在自己格内，横扫会侵入相邻帧。

CRITICAL BACKGROUND REQUIREMENT, THIS OVERRIDES EVERYTHING ELSE: the background of the
image must be 100% solid flat MAGENTA, hex #FF00FF, bright pure pink-purple. NOT black. NOT
white. NOT grey. NOT a gradient. Every single pixel that is not part of the character must be
exactly magenta #FF00FF, including the areas between the cells. The background is a chroma
key and a black background makes the whole image unusable.

The attached reference image contains ONE SINGLE character frame. It defines only the
CHARACTER IDENTITY and the ART STYLE. It does NOT define the layout of the image you must
produce. Do NOT copy the reference's framing, and do NOT produce a walk cycle or a 4x4
sheet — produce the 2x2 attack sheet described below.

Match the reference exactly on: the same chibi knight, same crimson red body, same
silver-white breastplate, same warm brown hair, same tan boots, same huge broad silver
greatsword with a straight crossguard, same thick near-black outlines, same flat fills with
light cel shading, same 3/4 top-down camera, same giant-head-tiny-body proportions.
Only the pose changes.

A 2x2 sprite sheet: 4 frames of a single two-handed greatsword ATTACK, the character FACING
DOWN toward the camera the whole time. The swing is a COMPACT OVERHEAD CHOP straight down
the character's own centerline, NOT a wide horizontal sweep.

FRAME ORDER, reading left-to-right then top-to-bottom:
  Frame 1 (top-left):     WIND-UP. Greatsword raised straight UP above the head, blade
                          vertical and pointing at the sky, both hands on the grip, knees
                          bent, torso coiled.
  Frame 2 (top-right):    CHOP. Blade swung down to about chest height in front of the body,
                          still steeply angled, torso leaning forward.
  Frame 3 (bottom-left):  IMPACT. Blade driven DOWN in front of the feet, tip pointing at the
                          ground, arms extended downward, torso lunged forward. Strongest,
                          most extreme pose.
  Frame 4 (bottom-right): RECOVERY. Blade lifted back to a neutral guard beside the body,
                          angled up, torso straightening.

ABSOLUTE RULES:
1. Background is 100% solid flat magenta #FF00FF everywhere, including between the cells.
   No gradient, no vignette, no ground shadow, no drop shadow, no black.
2. EXACTLY 4 equal-size cells in a 2x2 grid. Every cell the same width and height.
3. NO borders, NO dividing lines, NO gutters, NO frames between cells. Do not draw any line
   where the cells meet. Cells are separated only by empty magenta background.
4. IDENTICAL BODY SCALE and IDENTICAL camera distance in all 4 cells. Measure him from the
   top of his hair to the soles of his boots, ignoring the sword: that height must be the
   same in all 4 cells. Do not zoom in on the action.
5. His BODY (hair-top to boots, sword excluded) fills only about 40% of the cell height,
   centered in its cell, leaving a very wide empty magenta margin on all four sides.
   Draw it small. A small character with a lot of empty margin is CORRECT.
6. NOTHING may touch or cross a cell edge or the outer edge of the image — not the blade
   tip, not the hair, not a boot. There must be clearly visible magenta between the blade
   tip and every edge in all 4 frames. Keep the sword inside the character's own vertical
   column — never let the blade reach out sideways past his shoulders. If a pose does not
   fit, make the character smaller.
7. Feet rest on the same horizontal baseline in every cell. The body may bob and lunge, but
   do not let the character float or leave the ground.
8. Draw the CHARACTER BODY ONLY. NO motion-blur streaks, NO slash arc, NO energy trail, NO
   impact flash, NO dust, NO sparks, NO hit effect of any kind. The swing VFX is drawn
   separately by the engine.
9. NO text of any kind anywhere in the image. Do NOT write the frame names, do NOT caption
   the cells, no labels, no numbers, no arrows, no UI, no watermark.
