A 4x4 sprite sheet showing a full 4-direction walk cycle for a top-down tactics RPG unit. Keep
the character in the reference image exactly: same stout chibi shield bearer, same steel-blue
armour and blue hood, same cream off-white rectangular tower shield with a bold blue cross on it,
same thick near-black outlines, same flat fills with light cel shading, same palette, same
giant-head-tiny-body proportions. Only the facing direction and the walk pose change between cells.

HIS TOWER SHIELD IS HIS WHOLE IDENTITY. It is a big rectangular slab, WIDER THAN HIS OWN BODY and
nearly as tall as he is, cream off-white with one bold blue cross. He carries it on one arm out to
his SIDE, held slightly forward — never flat across his front, because his legs must stay visible
so the walk reads. In the back view the shield is still clearly visible past his silhouette.

IDENTICAL BODY SCALE in all 16 cells: measure him from the top of his head to the soles of his
boots, ignoring the shield — that height must be the same in all 16 cells. Same camera distance
in every cell.

PROPORTIONS: extreme super-deformed mascot. His head including hood is about HALF of his body
height; body, arms and legs together are the other half — short, stubby, chunky, almost no neck.
His body, head-top to boots and not counting the shield, fits in a roughly SQUARE box. Not a
2-head or 2.5-head chibi; think 1.5-head mascot.

SIMPLIFY: only 4 to 6 flat colours. STEEL BLUE is his identity colour and covers most of his body;
the cream shield face is his one light area, reading as near-white. No belts, straps, buckles,
pouches, rivets, armour seams, laces, stitching or trim. Face is minimal: two simple dark eyes,
two eyebrows, at most a tiny mouth line. Arms and legs are simple rounded stubs; hands are mitten
shapes with no fingers. Nothing on the shield except the single blue cross.

ART STYLE: hand-drawn cartoon game sprite, thick near-black outlines, flat saturated fills with
light cel shading (one highlight tone, one shadow tone), no gradients, no texture noise, no
painterly rendering. Bright saturated palette with a genuine near-white highlight.

CAMERA: 3/4 top-down view from slightly above, consistent in all 16 cells.

SHEET LAYOUT — rows are facing direction:
  Row 1 (top):    facing DOWN toward the camera, face fully visible
  Row 2:          facing LEFT, left side profile
  Row 3:          facing RIGHT, right side profile, mirrored from row 2
  Row 4 (bottom): facing UP away from the camera, back of the head and armour visible — the tower
                  shield must still be plainly visible at his side

ABSOLUTE RULES:
1. EXACTLY 16 equal-size cells in a 4x4 grid, every cell the same width and height.
2. NO borders, NO dividing lines, NO frames between cells — cells separated only by flat
   background colour.
3. Background is 100% solid flat pure magenta #FF00FF everywhere including between cells.
   No gradient, no vignette, no shadow.
4. IDENTICAL character identity, costume and palette in all 16 cells.
5. His BODY (head-top to boots, shield excluded) fills about 45% of the cell height in EVERY cell.
   The shield extends past that.
6. Nothing crosses a cell edge.
7. Feet rest on the same horizontal baseline in every cell — the body bobs down by squashing,
   not by floating.
8. NO text, NO labels, NO numbers, NO arrows, NO UI, NO watermark anywhere.
