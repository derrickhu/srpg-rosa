CRITICAL BACKGROUND REQUIREMENT, THIS OVERRIDES EVERYTHING ELSE: the background of the
image must be 100% solid flat MAGENTA, hex #FF00FF, bright pure pink-purple. NOT black. NOT
white. NOT grey. NOT a gradient. Every single pixel that is not part of the character must be
exactly magenta #FF00FF, including the areas between the cells. The background is a chroma
key and a black background makes the whole image unusable.

The attached reference image contains ONE SINGLE character frame. It defines only the
CHARACTER IDENTITY and the ART STYLE. It does NOT define the layout of the image you must
produce. Do NOT copy the reference's framing, and do NOT produce a walk cycle or a 4x4
sheet — produce the 2x2 attack sheet described below.

Match the reference exactly on: the same chibi boy priest, same cream-white robe, same gold
collar band, same mint-green sash, same warm brown hair, same tan boots, same thick brown
staff with a huge gold sun disc (circle plus six thick triangular rays), same thick
near-black outlines, same flat fills with light cel shading, same 3/4 top-down camera,
same giant-head-tiny-body proportions. Only the pose changes.

A 2x2 sprite sheet: 4 frames of a single STAFF THRUST attack. <朝向段落>. He jabs the sun staff
straight forward along his own centerline — NOT a wide horizontal sweep. The sun disc is the
thing that moves; the change between frames must be obvious in silhouette.

  朝下：the character FACING DOWN toward the camera the whole time. Face fully visible. He
        thrusts the staff FORWARD toward the viewer, sun disc leading, staying on his vertical
        centerline.
  朝左：the character seen in LEFT SIDE PROFILE, facing LEFT the whole time and thrusting toward
        the left. He stays in LEFT profile in all 4 frames — never turn him toward the camera,
        never flip him to face right.
  朝上：the character seen FROM BEHIND, facing UP and AWAY from the camera the whole time,
        thrusting away from the viewer. His face is never visible. The huge gold sun disc must
        still be plainly visible in every frame.

FRAME ORDER, reading left-to-right then top-to-bottom:
  Frame 1 (top-left):     WIND-UP. Staff pulled back beside his shoulder, sun disc near his
                          head, knees bent, torso coiled.
  Frame 2 (top-right):    THRUST. Staff driven forward, sun disc at about chest height in front
                          of the body, torso leaning into the jab.
  Frame 3 (bottom-left):  IMPACT. Full extension — the sun disc is rammed as far forward as it
                          goes, arms stretched, leading foot planted, torso lunged. Strongest
                          pose. Keep the staff on his own centerline; never let the sun reach
                          sideways past his shoulders.
  Frame 4 (bottom-right): RECOVERY. Staff lifted back to a neutral carry beside the body, sun
                          up, torso straightening.

ABSOLUTE RULES:
1. Background is 100% solid flat magenta #FF00FF everywhere, including between the cells.
   No gradient, no vignette, no ground shadow, no drop shadow, no black.
2. EXACTLY 4 equal-size cells in a 2x2 grid. Every cell the same width and height.
3. NO borders, NO dividing lines, NO gutters, NO frames between cells.
4. IDENTICAL BODY SCALE and IDENTICAL camera distance in all 4 cells. Measure him from the
   top of his hair to the soles of his boots, ignoring the staff: that height must be the
   same in all 4 cells. Do not zoom in on the action.
5. His BODY (hair-top to boots, staff excluded) fills only about 40% of the cell height,
   centered in its cell, leaving a very wide empty magenta margin on all four sides.
   Draw it small. A small character with a lot of empty margin is CORRECT.
6. NOTHING may touch or cross a cell edge — not the sun rays, not a boot. Keep the staff
   inside the character's own vertical column. If a pose does not fit, make the character smaller.
7. Feet rest on the same horizontal baseline in every cell.
8. Draw the CHARACTER BODY ONLY. NO motion-blur streaks, NO energy trail, NO holy VFX, NO
   spell circle, NO impact flash, NO dust, NO sparks. The spell VFX is drawn separately.
9. NO purple, magenta, pink, or violet anywhere on the character (the background magenta is
   the key colour, not a costume colour).
10. NO text of any kind anywhere in the image. No labels, no numbers, no arrows, no UI, no watermark.
