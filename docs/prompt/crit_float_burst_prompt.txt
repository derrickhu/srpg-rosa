A SINGLE GAME UI ICON. One icon only, centred, filling about 65% of the frame, with a clear magenta margin on all four sides.

CRITICAL BACKGROUND: the entire background, including all space around the icon, must be 100% solid flat MAGENTA, hex #FF00FF. It is a chroma key and will be deleted. No white, no grey, no gradient, no drop shadows onto the background, no glow bleeding into the magenta.

CRITICAL SIZE CONSTRAINT: this mark is displayed at only 16 to 22 pixels beside a damage number. It must be ONE bold, chunky, instantly readable silhouette. NO thin lines, NO tiny sparks detached from the body, NO text, NO numbers.

CRITICAL CONNECTIVITY: ONE single connected shape. No floating sparkles beside the burst.

This is a floating-damage PREFIX MARK, not a card icon. Think of a comic-book HIT SPARK stuck to the left of a fat outlined number, like a sticker.

SUBJECT — a compact jagged IMPACT BURST / hit spark:
- 4 to 6 irregular triangular points, slightly uneven lengths, like a hand-drawn POW explosion. NOT a regular 8-point star, NOT a symmetric badge.
- Tight and dense: the mass sits in the centre, points are short and fat, not long needles.
- Thick dark reddish-brown outline (#5A1408), same weight as outlined damage numbers. The outline is part of the sticker, not a drop shadow.
- Interior colour steps (cel-shaded, not painterly): deep crimson just inside the outline → saturated orange → bright golden yellow core → one tiny white/cream 4-point glint fused in the very centre.
- Warm hot colours only. Absolutely NO purple, NO violet, NO magenta in the icon itself.

It must read as "a critical hit spark next to a damage number", NOT as:
- a diagonal X or plus
- a sword or skull
- a purple starburst
- a fat toy hex-burst badge for a skill-mod card
- an additive VFX glow blob

NO text, no labels, no captions, no letters, no numbers, no watermark anywhere in the image.
