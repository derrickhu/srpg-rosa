# 单位 bow（弓兵职业） —— 四方向行走 sheet（generate2dsprite player_sheet, 4x4）
#
# 风格基准：godot/art/units/sword/ 现有单位（SD 卡通、粗黑描边、扁平上色 + 轻微 cel 阴影、
# 3/4 俯视）。不要像素风。
# 键色：#FF00FF —— 角色主色为绿/棕/皮革，与品红色相距远，不会被抠掉。
# 下游：generate2dsprite.py process --target player --mode player_sheet --align feet
#       --shared-scale --component-mode largest

A 4x4 sprite sheet showing a full 4-direction walk cycle for a top-down tactics RPG unit.

CHARACTER: a forest ranger. Chibi / super-deformed proportions with a large head and short
body, roughly 2.5 heads tall. Hooded green cloak with a lighter green lining, brown leather
armor and bracers, tan boots, a short recurve bow held in the screen-left hand, a quiver on
the back. Warm brown hair, simple determined face with thick dark eyes.

ART STYLE: hand-drawn cartoon game sprite, thick dark brown outlines, flat saturated fill
colors with light cel shading, no texture noise, no gradients, no painterly rendering, no
pixel art, no anti-aliased blur. Read cleanly at small size.

CAMERA: 3/4 top-down view from slightly above, consistent in all 16 cells. You can see the
top of the head, the shoulders, and the full body down to the boots.

SHEET LAYOUT — rows are facing direction, columns are walk frames:
  Row 1 (top):    facing DOWN, toward the camera, face fully visible
  Row 2:          facing LEFT, left side profile
  Row 3:          facing RIGHT, right side profile, mirrored from row 2
  Row 4 (bottom): facing UP, away from the camera, back of the hood and quiver visible
  Column 1: neutral standing pose, both feet together
  Column 2: LEFT foot stepping forward, slight body bob down
  Column 3: neutral standing pose, both feet together
  Column 4: RIGHT foot stepping forward, slight body bob down

ABSOLUTE RULES:
1. EXACTLY 16 equal-size cells in a 4x4 grid. Every cell the same width and height.
2. NO borders, NO dividing lines, NO frames between cells. Cells are separated only by the
   flat background color.
3. Background is 100% solid flat magenta #FF00FF everywhere, including between the cells.
   No gradient, no vignette, no ground shadow, no drop shadow under the character.
4. IDENTICAL SCALE in all 16 cells: same head-to-boot height, same shoulder width, same
   camera distance. Do not zoom in or out between cells. Do not crop tighter in some cells.
5. IDENTICAL character identity, costume, and palette in all 16 cells.
6. Character is centered in its cell and fills about 65% of the cell height, leaving even
   magenta margin on all four sides.
7. Nothing crosses a cell edge — no hair, cloak, bow, quiver, arrow, boot, or hand may touch
   or overlap a neighboring cell.
8. Feet rest on the same horizontal baseline in every cell.
9. NO text, NO labels, NO numbers, NO arrows, NO UI, NO watermark anywhere in the image.
