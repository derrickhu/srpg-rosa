Top-down bird's eye view of a forest clearing for a mobile game battlefield background.

Composition (portrait orientation, 3:4 aspect ratio):
- The entire image is viewed from DIRECTLY ABOVE, looking straight down. NO sky, NO horizon, NO perspective vanishing point.
- CENTER: A large open grass field area (about 60% of the image), bright warm green color, with subtle grass texture variations. This is where the game grid will overlay.
- EDGES (all four sides): Dense deciduous trees and bushes viewed from above, forming a natural border around the clearing. Trees should have round, lush canopies in various shades of green (lime, emerald, olive). Some trees slightly overlap into the grass area for a natural look.
- CORNERS: Slightly denser tree clusters.
- Scattered small details near the tree edges: tiny wildflowers (yellow, white), small rocks, fallen leaves. Keep these at the very periphery, NOT in the central grass area.

Art style:
- Simplified cartoon style with clean shapes and bright, comfortable colors
- Soft shading, no harsh shadows
- Color palette: warm greens (#7EC87E to #5A8A3A), with pops of yellow-green and olive
- The grass in the center should be a consistent bright green that will blend with game terrain tiles
- Trees should look like round puffy canopies from above
- Overall feel: cheerful, inviting, like a peaceful forest glade

Technical requirements:
- NO TEXT, no labels, no captions, no writing anywhere in the image
- NO grid lines, NO UI elements
- The central grass area should be clean and uncluttered (game tiles will cover it)
- Seamless transition from grass to tree edges (no hard borders)
- High detail at edges, minimal detail in center
