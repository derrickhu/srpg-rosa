A visual-only character concept sheet for advanced professions in a bright cute casual mobile SRPG.

Create a 6 columns x 2 rows grid, 12 cells total. Each column is one advanced profession, and each row is a different independent character style for that profession. These are different people, not palette swaps.

No text in the image. Do not write profession names, labels, captions, letters, numbers, UI, or frame marks.

GLOBAL STYLE:
- Cute chibi / SD fantasy characters, bright mobile RPG look.
- Thick dark outlines, rounded toy-like shapes, simple readable silhouettes.
- Bright saturated colors with soft one-step cel shading.
- Keep details simple; no realism, no dark gritty style, no pixel art, no complex texture.
- Slight top-down RPG map angle, suitable for small unit sprites.
- Each cell has one character centered on solid light gray background (#DDDDDD).
- No shadows, no ground ellipse, no magical background effects.
- Each character fills about 75-80% of its cell height.

COLUMN ORDER:
Column 1: Dual Blade Swordsman, fast melee attacker with two small blades.
Column 2: Sniper, long-range archer with larger bow or compact crossbow-like bow.
Column 3: Dragon Rider, cute flying charge unit on a tiny dragon mount.
Column 4: Iron Wall, ultra-heavy defender with huge shield and heavy armor.
Column 5: Ice Mage, control mage with ice staff and cold color palette.
Column 6: Poisoner, damage-over-time alchemist/rogue with vial or short staff.

ROW STYLE THEMES:
Row 1: clean heroic versions with clear profession identity and bright approachable colors.
Row 2: more eccentric mascot/fantasy versions with stronger silhouettes and unusual palettes.

PROFESSION VISUAL RULES:
- Dual Blade Swordsman: two small blades visible; agile stance; no big shield.
- Sniper: ranged weapon clearly visible; focused pose; slim readable silhouette.
- Dragon Rider: mount and rider both visible but simple; no complex wings covering the rider.
- Iron Wall: biggest shield and sturdiest body in the sheet; strong defensive shape.
- Ice Mage: icy blue/white/lavender cues; staff or wand; no large spell effects.
- Poisoner: green/purple vial cues; sneaky alchemist silhouette; no gore or scary realism.

IMPORTANT:
- Every cell must be a unique independent character.
- Do not use the same palette as the current shield-guard set for every design.
- Keep a unified cute casual game style while making each profession visually distinct.

ABSOLUTE TEXT BAN: NO TEXT, NO WORDS, NO LETTERS, NO NUMBERS, NO CAPTIONS, NO LABELS, NO WRITING ANYWHERE.
