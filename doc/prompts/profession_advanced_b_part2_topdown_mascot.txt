A pure visual-only character concept sheet for a bright cute casual mobile SRPG.

Create a 3 columns x 2 rows grid, 6 cells total. Each cell contains exactly one independent tiny top-down profession character on a solid light gray background (#DDDDDD).

ABSOLUTE TEXT BAN: no text, no words, no letters, no numbers, no captions, no labels, no title, no UI, no typography anywhere.

CRITICAL PROPORTION RULES:
- Super-deformed mascot map-unit proportions.
- Total character height is only about 1 to 1.5 head-heights tall.
- Very large round head, tiny compact body, tiny feet, tiny arms.
- Do NOT draw tall anime proportions. These are tiny cute map units.

CRITICAL CAMERA RULES:
- Clear top-down / slightly tilted bird's-eye RPG map view.
- Show the top surface of head, helmet, hood, mount back, shoulders, shield, staff, weapon, and feet placement.
- NOT front portrait, NOT side-scroller, NOT isometric diamond.

GLOBAL STYLE:
- Bright cute mobile RPG style, thick dark outlines, rounded toy-like shapes.
- Bright saturated colors with one simple shadow color.
- No cast shadows, no ground ellipses, no background effects.
- Keep details very simple and readable.

CELL DESIGNS, row-major order:
1. Tiny fast raider riding a small brown wolf, green headband, short spear.
2. Tiny holy shield healer, blonde hair, white-gold armor, small staff, sun shield.
3. Tiny young bone mage, dark robe, tiny skull staff, cute spooky but harmless.
4. Tiny orange tribal rider on fluffy white wolf, fur hood, small axe.
5. Tiny green-gold holy guardian, heart-shaped shield, small mace, teal and coral accents.
6. Tiny purple witchy summoner, wide hat, tiny skull charm, bone wand.

Every character must be a different independent person with distinct silhouette and palette.
