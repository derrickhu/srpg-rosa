A visual-only character concept sheet for a bright cute casual mobile SRPG.

Create a 6 columns x 3 rows grid, 18 cells total. Each column is one profession, and each row is a different independent character style for that profession. These are different people, not the same person changing clothes.

No text in the image. Do not write profession names, labels, captions, letters, numbers, UI, or frame marks.

GLOBAL STYLE:
- Cute chibi / SD fantasy characters for a casual mobile RPG.
- Bright cheerful palette, thick dark outline, rounded shapes, soft one-step cel shading.
- Simple readable silhouettes, no tiny details, no noisy texture, no realism, no pixel art.
- Slight top-down RPG map character angle; show a little top of head/helmet, shoulders, weapon top edge, and feet.
- Each cell has one character centered on solid light gray background (#DDDDDD).
- No shadows, no ground ellipse, no effects behind character.
- Each character fills about 75-80% of its cell height.

COLUMN ORDER:
Column 1: Swordsman, melee sword unit.
Column 2: Archer, ranged bow unit.
Column 3: Cavalry, cute mounted charge unit.
Column 4: Shield Guard, defensive shield unit.
Column 5: Mage, magical ranged unit.
Column 6: Priest, healer support unit.

ROW STYLE THEMES:
Row 1: bright starter-adventurer designs, friendly heroic silhouettes, warm accessible colors.
Row 2: nature / animal / mascot-inspired designs, playful distinct silhouettes, fresh greens, tans, coral, aqua, or cream colors.
Row 3: magical / elite / chapter-reward designs, more special color palettes such as lavender, turquoise, gold, white, red, or navy, but still simple.

PROFESSION VISUAL RULES:
- Swordsman: small sword must be visible; agile close-combat stance; no shield dominance.
- Archer: bow must be visible and readable; quiver or arrow cue; slim silhouette.
- Cavalry: rider on a small cute mount or pony; compact enough to fit cell; lance or small spear cue.
- Shield Guard: shield must be dominant; small weapon; sturdy body.
- Mage: staff or wand must be visible; robe or hat silhouette; magical but no big effects.
- Priest: healing staff, holy symbol, soft robe; gentle support silhouette; no attack-heavy pose.

IMPORTANT:
- Every cell must be a unique independent character design.
- Do not reuse the same palette across all professions.
- Keep the overall art direction consistent with bright cute mobile RPG screenshots.
- Designs should be suitable for later expansion into animation frames.

ABSOLUTE TEXT BAN: NO TEXT, NO WORDS, NO LETTERS, NO NUMBERS, NO CAPTIONS, NO LABELS, NO WRITING ANYWHERE.
