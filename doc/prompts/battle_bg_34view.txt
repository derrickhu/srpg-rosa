A mobile game battlefield background in 3/4 top-down perspective (slightly tilted bird's eye view, about 60 degree angle from horizontal), PORTRAIT orientation (taller than wide, 9:16 ratio).

Composition:
- The viewpoint looks down at the ground at a slight angle, so you can see the FRONT of trees and objects slightly (not pure top-down).
- TOP area: Dense conifer/pine trees in the background, smaller due to perspective. Mix of dark green pine trees and some deciduous trees. Trees are shown from a slight above-and-in-front angle so you see their tops and a bit of their front sides.
- LEFT and RIGHT sides: Trees and bushes along both edges, getting larger toward the bottom (closer to camera). Tree trunks partially visible.
- CENTER: Large open grassy clearing (about 55% of image area). Bright warm green grass with subtle texture. This is where the game grid will be placed, so keep it clean.
- BOTTOM area: Larger foreground trees and bushes (closest to camera), showing more of their front/trunk. Some grass, small flowers, mushrooms near tree bases.
- The ground/grass should have a very subtle perspective - slightly lighter/smaller texture at top, slightly darker/larger at bottom.

Art style:
- Simplified cartoon game style, clean outlines, bright comfortable colors
- Similar to casual mobile strategy/tower defense games
- Color palette: warm greens for grass (#7EC87E), darker greens for trees (#3A7A3A to #5A9A4A), brown tree trunks
- Soft lighting from above, gentle shadows under trees
- Cheerful forest glade atmosphere

DO NOT include:
- NO sky visible (the camera angle is steep enough that only ground and tree canopies fill the frame)
- NO TEXT, no labels, no captions, no writing
- NO grid lines, NO UI elements, NO characters
- NO buildings or man-made structures
